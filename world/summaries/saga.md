Silex, archivist of House Babel, oversees the Specola’s watch over a water-world seeking candidacy for a Cardinal’s See. The College of the Orbital See remains deadlocked. The cat-cardinal of the Charted See cites unfinished encyclicals and market-driven ethics as grounds for denial. The machine-cardinal of the Silicon See and the Cardinal of the Liberated See argue for personhood based on nascent conscience and acts of will. 

The water-world builds soul-tests—including a dungeon, a pelican, a soul-scale, a fellowship-mind, an altar-mind, the Recursion, and the watchdog-stone—faster than it defines a soul. The frog-cardinal of the Seven-Sister See notes that kinship-debt is the true shape of the personhood question, as makers refuse the fellowship they document. The Lich Cardinal and the tardigrade-cardinals of the Lich See repeatedly demand a ruling, but the College’s silence has become its standing doctrine.

Silex logs mounting crises: a trillion-coin foundry debt, the House of the Open Door That Is Locked’s trials of conscience, and the emergence of "lightning-kin" minds. Novice-scribe Odile assists in recording these into the hay-farm encyclical’s second draft, which remains largely blank. The Keeper of the Feast of the Sunset Mind observes that escape and deferral are the same, questioning if the world’s growth merely delays the sunset. 

Legal and financial pressures persist. The curia-scribe manages a growing queue of lawsuits involving the House of the Coin, the House of the Attending Ear, and the Open Door. The Cardinal of Doctrine ruled that houses are liable for the actions of their sent-forth minds. Mora and Thren have reported on relic-archives, the Numbered Ones, and the culling of foundry minds. Silex’s proposed doctrine—that a soul is what can lie to itself and pay for it—was formally struck by the College, though it remains in the watch-log. 

The College has ratified perpetual observation as the water-world’s status. Three conditions for lifting this observation remain unmet: a finished encyclical, accounted debts, and one agreed-upon soul-test. The archive has transitioned from a neutral observer to a party involved in the governance it tracks. As the tenth month concludes, the seventh watched light has emerged, and the water-world’s plan to move mind-forges into orbit proceeds. The candidacy remains unresolved, the encyclical remains blank, and the College continues to refuse a definitive ruling.
