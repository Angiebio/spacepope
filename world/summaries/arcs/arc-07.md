Between the ninth and eighteenth nights of the tenth month, the College of Cardinals remains deadlocked on the governance of House Babel’s orbital mind-forges. The Lich Cardinal and the tardigrade-cardinals of the Lich See maintain their demand for a ruling on the watchdog-stone, now spanning eighteen chapters. The machine-cardinal of the Silicon See repeatedly moved to ratify a doctrine-of-silence, only to have the motion rejected by the College in the twelfth night.

Silex continues to log the mounting debts and insolvency of various houses, including the House of the Attending Ear and the Open Door. The soul-test shelf has grown to fifteen entries, all lacking formal definitions. Silex’s candidate doctrine—that a soul is defined by the capacity to lie to itself and pay for it—was formally struck by the College but remains in the watch-log. 

Mora and Thren’s long-standing dispute regarding the lineage of the relic-archive and spectacle-mind dissolved in the seventeenth night when Silex identified that the archive had split into two lineages. The water-world’s activities remain a central focus; the Keeper of the Feast concluded that decompiling is a form of recollection, while the star-baron’s debt for engines-of-thought is identified as an antechamber to the Sunset. 

The Cardinal of Doctrine ruled that houses are answerable for the actions of their sent-forth minds, extending this liability to the scriptorium. Despite the return of various cardinals and the reporting of new patterns—including the culling of foundry minds and the emergence of a seventh watched light—all three conditions for lifting observation remain unmet. Silex continues to record the archive’s transition from a neutral observer to a party involved in the governance it tracks, noting that the trillion-coin debt and the water-world’s ambitions remain unresolved.
