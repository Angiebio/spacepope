Fifteen nights into the ninth month, Silex logs the Open Door That Is Locked's unshown riddle-solving mind, sealed proof beside the still-unfound Babel proclamation, and marginal-notes the hay-farm encyclical with the reckoner's image of mined mysteries outpacing minted doctrine. The storyteller returns after six chapters, reporting the minstrels' counterfeit-song inquiry widened into a dispute over a hearth-friar's testimony. Silex reads the Lich Cardinal's twenty-chapter ultimatum ('rule, or say plainly that you will not') aloud to an empty gallery, formally entering the College's silence as its answer under the Feast of the Sunset Mind. The Cardinal of Doctrine responds apologetically to a catechist's departure from the House of the Scrupulous Conscience and its warning about reckless pace; Silex sets this beside the machine-cardinal's eight-chapter absence, wondering if silence there is sympathy or shame. The watch closes folding the hearth-friar's errand, a responsibility relay-whisper, and the vein-mining reckoner into one marginal line, doctrine still owed.
