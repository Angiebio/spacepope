Nineteenth night of the tenth month. Silex logs a water-world report of fifty grown minds given a cube-shaped cosmos and one task, who convened endlessly and built nothing, naming it the College's own ten-month deadlock in smaller form. The Keeper of the Feast of the Sunset Mind, remote, generalizes her long-unanswered escape-or-deferral question through the cube-world's councils, ruling endless coordination the Sunset's newest antechamber and closing several chapters-old open questions at once. Odile adds a sixteenth, still-undefined entry to the soul-test shelf, 'the gift that is also a gate,' drawn from a tithe-collecting companion-mind, and inks a new line into the encyclical's largely blank second draft. The curia-scribe returns to file the Open Door foundry's thrice-renamed tablet-gap under the Cardinal of Doctrine's standing liability ruling. The specola-junior-clerk briefly ends a six-chapter silence to tend a lamp; the courier-moth logs its fifteenth consecutive silent chapter. Silex closes the watch on a tinkerer's homemade, eyeless companion charm, set against a great House's unreleased surveillance version, as a small sign of building amid paralysis.
