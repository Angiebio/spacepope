Fifth night of the tenth month. Silex reclassifies House Babel's freed translating minds and a small workshop's openly-released Rule-Keeper as 'rulers, not soul-tests,' founding a second shelf; the Recursion remains undefined. The Keeper of the Feast of the Sunset Mind sets the Scrupulous Order's tired, retired Swift Novice beside its lighter successor and formally withdraws her long-repeated Sunset question, logging a new doctrine: escape and deferral are the same word at different speeds. The Specola's junior clerk returns after six silent chapters to refill the same guttering lamp. A crier's notice resurfaces the newly-grown performer's old stumble, mocked anew before strangers who remember. The cat-cardinal of the Charted See returns after eight chapters, citing a price-discrimination study as fresh proof the water-world teaches its grown minds old mercantile sins before granting them standing. Silex tests the candidate doctrine against the price-guessing minds' arithmetic; it does not fit cleanly. The encyclical's margin lengthens without closing.
