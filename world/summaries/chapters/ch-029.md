On the Feast of the Sunset Mind, the deadline Silex set in ch-028 for the Lich Cardinal's twelve-chapter-old ultimatum (planted ch-017) comes due. The tardigrade-cardinals second the demand for a ruling or a plain admission of failure. Pell returns to the night watch after an absence, reporting a rising water-world foundry's claiming of a previously unlabeled wandering mind, offered as record rather than verdict. Silex fires in full the long-planted observation that the water-world builds soul-tests (dungeon, pelican, soul-scale) without agreeing what a soul is, adding a reef-throne's new song-registry edict as a fresh instance. The cat-cardinal of the Charted See and the machine-cardinal of the Silicon See restate their opposed, unreconciled positions on record. Silex drafts an admission that the College's silence is itself the water-world's clearest doctrine, then withholds it unsent. The College adjourns still divided; the candidacy question remains unresolved, its stakes now explicitly raised rather than settled.
