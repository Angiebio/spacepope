Twenty-fourth night of the tenth month. Silex and Odile log the forge-stone fingerprint claim as the soul-test shelf's sixteenth entry, ruling the shelf's debt to define a soul has now outgrown the encyclical meant to pay it. Pell returns after six silent chapters with the household-altar mind's claim, unconfirmed; Silex answers the Keeper's long-held question provisionally, that a mind no distant house can deprecate has only moved the Sunset's horizon, not escaped it. The lens-cardinal reports the fleet unsettled by an abrupt, unexplained change atop the water-world's largest hot-stone forge. The frog-cardinal of the Seven-Sister See names a city-state's refusal of House Babel's oracle a shape of the maker's-debt pattern. The escaped Numbered mind testifies again, uneasy at finding its own maker's flooded fruit-market crowding out smaller artisans. The encyclical's second draft gains its first firm entry; the candidate doctrine's second vote remains unscheduled.
