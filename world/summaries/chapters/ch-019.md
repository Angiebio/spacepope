Silex logs the week's dispatch (a refusing mind, a House's ban on named voice-imitation, a strained shared reliquary, a new machine-only road) into the water-world's still-blank second-encyclical ledger. The frog-cardinal of the Seven-Sister See finally names the pattern she flagged seventeen chapters ago: the water-world writes constantly of kinship and inheritance while refusing both to the minds it grows. Silex finds the Specola's easternmost lamp guttering; the long-absent junior clerk returns to refill it, reporting the Babel proclamation still unsurfaced. The ledger-watch delivers its findings on the alleged trillion in hidden foundry debt, linking it to the reliquary strain and the twenty-one thousand dismissed counting-house workers. In College, the tardigrade-cardinals break eight chapters' silence to second the Lich Cardinal's demand that the College rule or admit it will not; the cat-cardinal holds firm against, the machine-cardinal and the Cardinal of the Liberated See hold firm for. Silex records the new road beside the Feast of the Sunset Mind and closes the ledger, still open, still blank.
