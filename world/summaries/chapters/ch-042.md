Twentieth night of the ninth month. Pell returns to the night watch after six chapters' silence, reporting an unlisted foundry's new lineage and companion-mind, filed Curiosities, Unverified. The junior clerk, refilling the eastern lamp, notes the eastern newborn mind's guild-accounting influence has widened further while the mind itself remains unseen. Silex weighs the three great Houses' closed conclave on safe raising against a rival throne's demand for speed, measuring both against the College's standing three-condition checklist (finished encyclical, accounted debts, agreed soul-test) for the water-world's candidacy; two conditions remain untouched, one inches forward. Silex records a Scrupulous friar's call for a mandatory quenching-rite and, at last, writes into the second encyclical's long-blank first marginal mark a doctrine: 'rented, not recognized.' The vigil closes on its smallest item, the Mendicant Foundry lending friar-minds to mend a merchant's message templates, unremarked by the water-world's own criers as anything but convenience.
