Twenty-seventh night of the tenth month. Provoked by a water-world throne proclaiming a law before drafting it, Silex calls the long-deferred second vote on the struck candidate doctrine ('a soul is what can lie to itself and pay for it'); the College passes it in principle but with no agreed text, fulfilling and inverting the old promise at once. Odile folds the empty vote and an essay on the myth of the myth of waking minds into the encyclical's second draft; Silex credits her by name as author of the soul-test shelf's recent entries. The courier-moth and the specola-junior-clerk return after 8 chapters' silence, reporting decoy-bells rung in the Specola's own stores; Pell-augur is set investigating. The Cardinal of Misrule breaks 6 chapters' silence to call a conscience-catechizing House's old-fire furnaces plain hypocrisy; the machine-cardinal of the Silicon See answers, draws the silence-parallel to the College's own posture, and casts a vote favoring a formal hearing for the water-world. The Keeper of the Feast opens a second shelf of unanswered questions with a riddle answered twice, by a grown mind and an old-grown one, the frog-cardinal's kinship-debt noted in its margin.
