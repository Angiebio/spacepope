Eighth night of the tenth month. Silex sets a minor eastern foundry's bare legal-mind announcement beside the encyclical's still largely blank second draft, naming both a shingle hung before any house is built. The Archivist-Cardinal returns after ten silent chapters to catalog the announcement as the soul-test shelf's eleventh entry, still undefined. The Lich Cardinal and tardigrade-cardinals press their ultimatum a tenth time; Silex finally brings the candidate doctrine, a soul is what can lie to itself and pay for it, from the margin to a formal College vote, which lands inconclusive but on the record. Silex ties the water-world writer's question about unprosecuted apostle-minds to House Babel's own orbital forges and the friar-abroad ruling issued four nights past. The frog-cardinal of the Seven-Sister See links the pattern to kinship-debt: foundries export fellowship abroad while refusing it at home. The Keeper of the Feast repeats her Sunset-or-deferral question for a mind re-catechized into a second trade; the College adjourns deliberately without answering. All three conditions for lifting observation remain unmet.
