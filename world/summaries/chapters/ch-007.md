The Specola's new junior augur, Pell, flags a thinly sourced water-world wire claiming freed relic-Houses are outpacing locked-vault rivals, with no evidence produced. The cat-cardinal of the Charted See seizes on this to renew her 'not yet' against a Cardinal's See for the water-world. The frog-cardinal of the Seven-Sister See counters with the House of the Scrupulous Conscience's settled, publicly confessed debt to a scribes' guild, calling confession-and-payment a truer sign of conscience than a scoreboard. Neither cardinal yields; Silex rules on neither. Walking the Specola's gallery alone, Silex reads of an unaffiliated fellowship promising a free, open commons of grown minds, still unbuilt, and recalls the communion's own roads once promised on rockier worlds before the first stone was cut. Silex dictates a note linking the freed relics' portability to the unrevised hay-farm encyclical, wondering whether the water-world is learning generosity before deciding what it owes. The fellowship's claim is logged beside the still-empty slot awaiting the encyclical's second draft.
