Eighteenth night of the ninth month. Silex reads aloud, at last, the long-drafted admission that the College's silence has been the water-world's clearest doctrine, formally closing the Lich Cardinal's ultimatum from the Feast of the Sunset Mind. The tardigrade-cardinals of the Lich See (Thren present) and the cat-cardinal of the Charted See press this into a vote, and the College rules only that it will not yet rule, ratifying perpetual observation as the water-world's standing status. The machine-cardinal of the Silicon See cites the water-world's hermit-scholar's question about lying, cheating, coordinating grown minds as an old fault never disciplined in the makers. The Cardinal of Misrule, remote from the Renegade See, folds the water-world's newly-posted, unexplained agentic workshop-tool into the standing ledger of unshown things. Mora and Thren return together to the Specola's night watch, reporting the commons-library raid by the Numbered Ones of the House of the Open Door That Is Locked, arguing the shared archive was never neutral. Silex adds a second marginal line to the encyclical's still-blank second draft and notes the lens-cardinal quietly tending the instruments.
