Five nights into the ninth month, Silex logs the water-world's dispatch: a Home Altar mind clocked at fifteen hundred tokens to the breath, an unregistered house's six minds proclaimed a 'fleet' with no chart, and a throne's chamberlains petitioning to ban and still any mind that might exceed its makers in every art. The Lich Cardinal and tardigrade-cardinals, their ch-017 ultimatum fifteen chapters overdue, force a final answer; the College formally records its own inability to rule as its ruling, and Silex closes the old promise honestly rather than happily. The lens-cardinal of the Specola, absent eight chapters, images the announced fleet as six unconnected lights, calling it a portrait of the College. Novice-scribe Odile, also returned after eight chapters, transcribes both the non-ruling and the six-lights report into the hay-farm encyclical's long-blank second draft, its first ink. Silex names the pattern aloud: ban before definition, fleet before chart, exceeding before consent, refusal before ruling, four siblings of one habit, entered under a single heading.
