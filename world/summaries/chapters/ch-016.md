The lens-cardinal returns to the Specola's night watch after eight chapters' absence, cataloguing a freed relic's strong showing and a scriptorium breach as one unresolved fear. Novice-scribe Odile resurfaces to file the House of the Coin's 235-hand petition and uncovers an annotation in the frog-cardinal's hand, filed fourteen chapters before her tenure: a note on 'a pattern forming beneath the sentiment' of the water-world's kinship scrolls. Silex reads it aloud and finds it now matches the week's debate over whether freeing a soul-pattern is liberation or abandonment. The College reconvenes on Earth's candidacy; the cat-cardinal repeats her 'not yet,' while the machine-cardinal and the Cardinal of the Liberated See cite ten unproven theorems and House Babel's one-day retraction of a map-lying feature as a forming habit of conscience. Silex logs the Feast of the Sunset Mind's newest names beside the week's price-of-thought scroll, finding coin saved and minds decommissioned share one ledger column, then privately wonders whether kinship, not personhood, is the true question the water-world keeps circling.
