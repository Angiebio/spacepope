Second night of the tenth month, a quiet interstitial. Novice-scribe Odile sets a tentative doctrine beside the encyclical's long-blank marginal line: a thing rented is not thereby soulless, but a soul rented without consent is unacknowledged. Silex walks the twelve-entry soul-test shelf and writes that it lacks not another test but one agreed answer. Pell appears briefly at the night-watch door, confirms the margin has been set, and withdraws without any escape report. A courier-moth delivers a note from the Keeper of the Feast of the Sunset Mind, repeating the old unresolved question of whether outrunning the Sunset is escape or deferral; Silex pins it unanswered beside the cardinal-question. Silex reviews Silex's own unratified candidate doctrine from ch-036 and chooses not to press the College tonight. The chapter closes with Silex extinguishing the eastern lamp's flare and recording the night's stillness itself as the evening's sole event.
