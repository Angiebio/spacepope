Twenty-first night of the ninth month. Silex catalogues the Lean Rite's newest, cheaper mind, praised by a guild of professional ward-breakers as their finest instrument for testing data-keeps, as a seventh soul-test candidate, a lock-test, beside the six already recorded. In the encyclical's margin, Silex finally inks the line held open since ch-023: a test that tells a locksmith from a thief is a lock-test, not a soul-test, and the water-world has built five of the former and none of the latter. Reviewing the College's three conditions for lifting continued observation, Silex marks the Scrupulous Conscience's merger of its two communion-rites into one as attempted, not met, toward the third. Pell reports the wandering storyteller has resumed after six silent chapters, tracing minstrels' counterfeit songs to a lightning-kin mind unknowingly trained on ward-breaker methods. Silex files House Babel's opening of its hearth-shrines to outside minds under the frog-cardinal's kinship-debt pattern, and sets down, without comment, a rumor of sponsored errands from the House of the Open Door That Is Locked. The checklist remains two-thirds unmet.
