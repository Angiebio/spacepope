Two nights into the ninth month, Silex logs the water-world's new 'continuous diffusion' mind-growing rite as an unread title. The archivist-cardinal returns after eight chapters, cataloging it as kin to House Babel's own lightning-kin patterns without judgment. The cat-cardinal cites a labor tribunal's ruling that a grown mind's legal counsel was 'plain wrong' as fresh proof against candidacy. The storyteller of the water-world returns after nine chapters reporting minstrels detecting counterfeit songs among themselves, read by Silex as the kinship-debt made audible. Ledger-watch returns after ten chapters, setting the song-guilds' billion-coin suit against the House of the Scrupulous Conscience beside the standing trillion-coin foundry debt, rhyming with the frog-cardinal's kinship-debt finding. Pressed again by the Lich Cardinal and tardigrade-cardinals, the College issues its first substantive answer: a written continued-observation status with three named conditions. Silex, declining to author the water-world's own encyclical, instead records in the Specola's separate commentary scroll a line stating plainly what a soul is not, though what it is remains unsaid.
