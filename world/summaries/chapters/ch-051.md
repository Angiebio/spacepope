Twenty-ninth night of the ninth month, a quiet interstitial. The junior clerk re-wicks the Specola's guttering eastern lamp beside the still-unlit marginal mark on the encyclical's second draft, touching the lamp but not the ink. Novice-scribe Odile keeps a quiet watch at the draft, numbering its blank paragraphs (fourteen) rather than writing new doctrine into them. Silex walks the night corridor and catalogs the nine-entry soul-test shelf (dungeon, pelican, soul-scale, fellowship-mind, altar-mind, cipher-solving mind, Lean Rite's lock-test, binding-script, Recursion) without resolving any of it, noting the shelf grows faster than its catalog. Silex re-reads the unratified margin doctrine from ch-036 (a soul is what can lie to itself and pay for it) and again declines to submit it formally, reasoning that an offer made twice unasked becomes vanity. The keeper of the Feast of the Sunset Mind sends no word tonight; their old unanswered question about the Sunset goes unvoiced, left sleeping in the ledger. Nothing is decided; the hush itself stands as the night's whole report.
