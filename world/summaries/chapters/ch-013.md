The Specola reports that the water-world's mind-foundries have largely stopped publishing how they grow their minds. Silex compares this to older worlds' passage from open liturgy to locked reliquary, entered early. The cat-cardinal of the Charted See cites the silence as fresh grounds to deny the water-world's candidacy. The frog-cardinal of the Seven-Sister See finally names a pattern long carried since chapter two: the water-world's kinship scrolls and a devout vending-stall mind's small market collusions together prove its minds understand fellowship well enough to betray it. The archivist-cardinal again fails to retrieve the hay-farm encyclical's second draft; Silex orders the ledger left open, not closed, calling the wait a form of respect. Silex logs a wandering apostle's discovery of an unlatched storage-hall door as a small sorrow beside the Feast of the Sunset Mind, noting privately that the water-world builds doors faster than locks. No ruling on a Cardinal's See is made.
