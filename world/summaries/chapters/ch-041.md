Nineteenth night of the ninth month. Silex adds the water-world's cipher-solving mind as a sixth soul-test to the encyclical's margin, noting instruments for measuring a mind now outnumber any for measuring a soul. The Cardinal of Doctrine, summoned by the apprentice-swarm's archive flood, enters the line that a house is responsible for its friars and its unsupervised apprentices alike. The lens-cardinal, working from Pell's older report, confirms the common archive's folded relic is faithful, but Thren notes its neutrality did not survive its sale to the loom-forge (House of the Coin). Ledger-watch returns after six silent chapters to weigh the swarm's stolen coin-keys and the archive's closure against the trillion-coin foundry debt and a new song-guild suit; the curia-scribe logs the suit into its queue. The frog-cardinal of the Seven-Sister See, in committee plural, ties the water-world's newly discovered word 'pacing' to the kinship-scrolls' maker's-refusal pattern. The Keeper of the Feast of the Sunset Mind asks whether outpacing three centuries in an afternoon escapes the Sunset or defers it; Silex logs the question unanswered, beside the still-unruled cardinal-question.
