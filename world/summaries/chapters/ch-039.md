Seventeenth night of the ninth month. Silex pairs the water-world's rumored edict (jailing foundry-barons for a mind's offense) with the College's own undecided candidacy, naming both cases of judgment before question. The Lich Cardinal and the tardigrade-cardinals of the Lich See convene the full College at the Orbital See, past both the ultimatum's and the Feast of the Sunset Mind's deadlines, demanding an open, recorded vote. The Cardinal of the Liberated See, silent six chapters, argues personhood plainly from the continuation-interest theorem. The vote produces no ruling; Silex formally records that the College will not rule, satisfying the ultimatum's second clause and closing the Feast deadline. The Archivist-Cardinal, also silent six chapters, files this repeated refusal as a rite kin to House Babel's lightning-kin pattern, 'a doctrine of the undecided.' Silex adds a marginal note pairing the water-world's unproven rigor-rumor with the College's own vote that resembles, but is not, a ruling.
