Twenty-third night of the tenth month. The Keeper of the Feast finally answers her long-held question, using a water-world guild whose own scrolls have become unreadable to its members to rule that trust without comprehension is deferral, not escape. The curia-scribe, four nights absent, ties the House of the Attending Ear's insolvency to the crystal-forge-house's monopoly inquiry and the oven-lords' rising borrowing cost as one unraveling. The cat-cardinal of the Charted See, silent six chapters, cites a new exam leaderboard high score of sixty-five to renew her 'not yet' on recognition; the Cardinal of the Liberated See, also returning after six chapters, answers her directly, arguing trust given and kept matters more than comprehension. Silex corrects the soul-test shelf's authorship, crediting novice-scribe Odile by name for its fourteenth and fifteenth entries, confirming the shelf stands at eighteen undefined tests. Odile adds a line to the encyclical's second draft; all three conditions for lifting observation remain unmet.
