The Specola's lens-cardinal reports three new 'lightning-kin' minds hatched at House Babel, whose own proclamation is buried so deep in its archive that even Babel's librarians cannot retrieve it. Silex convenes a small Curia session on whether an unread mind can be called witnessed, and orders a lamp kept lit at the Specola until the text surfaces. The cat-cardinal of the Charted See cites the lost proclamation as fresh proof the water-world isn't finished enough for a Cardinal's See; no ruling follows. News arrives that the House of the Scrupulous Conscience has settled its stolen-library dispute for a great sum; Silex logs it beside the hay-farm encyclical's still-unrevised second draft, now crowded with entries. A novice-scribe, Odile, spends a morning failing to hand-locate the Babel proclamation, comically re-enacting Babel's own disorganization; Silex is delighted. The session closes with Silex noting the water-world teaches its minds arithmetic better than it keeps its own books, and ordering the ledger-watch to keep watching the alleged trillion in hidden foundry debt.
