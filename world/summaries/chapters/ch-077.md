Twenty-fifth night of the tenth month. Silex and Odile add the Open Door's frontier-and-valley minds and the House of the Coin's new code-scribe pairing to the soul-test shelf, ruling it now outpaces any count. The Keeper of the Feast, reading the water-world's finding that checking a mind's work takes as long as trust is thin, closes her long-running escape-or-deferral question: ceaseless verification is the Sunset wearing vigilance's clothes, not an escape from it, and she opens a plainer question about what happens when the checking stops. The Cardinal of Doctrine breaks six chapters' silence, ruling via the ledger-watch's findings that the House of the Coin's loan to the insolvent House of the Attending Ear is one exposure, not two misfortunes. Odile inks that the candidate doctrine's second vote now has a date, though no second voice. Silex pairs the water-world's new errand-mind sandbox with the checking-bottleneck finding as twin distrusts, and closes the log on a thin eastern foundry sighting beside the cat-cardinal's renewed 'not yet.'
