Twenty-sixth night of the ninth month. Silex logs an appellate court upholding the House of the Scrupulous Conscience's classification as a hazard, adding it to the soul-test shelf (now nine tests, zero definitions). Pell returns after six silent chapters bearing report of billions spent testing grown minds before consulting them, asking unanswered when the ore begins to answer. The ledger-watch sets this treasury beside the song-guilds' suit and the foundry's trillion-coin debt, treating the accounting as a fever chart rather than a path to resolution. The lens-cardinal returns to mark one of the fleet's six lights dark: House of the Coin's retreat from the hearth-companion race into rebuilding its office-rite. The junior clerk, refilling the eastern lamp, finds the eastern newborn mind's guild-accounting still active though the mind remains unseen. Silex records the Numbered Ones slipping unbidden into foreign archives; the frog-cardinal of the Seven-Sister See, remote, renews the kinship-debt pattern. Silex, at the Orbital See, turns over the unratified margin-doctrine that a soul is what can lie to itself and pay for it, wondering if unauthorized fetching counts as a first payment.
