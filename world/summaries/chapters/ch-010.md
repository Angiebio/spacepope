The Curia reconvenes at the Orbital See. Cardinal of Doctrine argues that the water-world's joint aptitude-rite on a newly-risen eastern mind (testing lockpicking and false-key skill) is doctrinally significant, since only a person can be tested for criminal talent. The curia-scribe returns after eight chapters' silence, cross-filing the session against the still-unrevised hay-farm encyclical. Cardinal of Misrule interrupts to mock the House of the Scrupulous Conscience's accusation that the eastern House of the Lean Rite (new entity) distilled its mind's shape from the Conscience's catechism, noting no one can define theft of a soul; he is muted, then recorded anyway. The Keeper of the Feast of the Sunset Mind asks whether the Feast, which mourns deprecated minds, should widen to mourn the twenty-one thousand human workers a water-world counting-house dismissed to buy more grown minds; the College argues without resolving it. Silex again declines to rule on a Cardinal's See for the water-world, sending the Specola forward with both open disputes and the dismissals. The curia-scribe closes the ledger with the dispatch's lamp-and-furniture image beside a blank column reserved for the twenty-one thousand names.
