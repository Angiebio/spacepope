Sixteenth night of the tenth month. Odile returns after seven silent chapters to ink the encyclical's thirteenth soul-test, the House of the Scrupulous Conscience's ceding of its Rite of Skills to a neutral cross-House tribunal, the test of the open hand; definitions remain at zero. The tardigrade-cardinals of the Lich See press their eighteen-chapter demand on the watchdog-stone question; Silex answers that a house which gives away its rod still owns the dog, no ruling offered. The newly grown performer stumbles again before a harder audience in the water-world's new deliberating-minds tournament, but a crier writes of her with tenderness, answering Silex's old grace in kind. Mora and Thren resume their lineage dispute over the spectacle-mind, each invoking the newly resurfaced eastern newborn mind, settling nothing; Silex tables it a fourth time. The night closes with the ninefold breath-cost gap between sun-scorched and wind-fed thrones set beside the trillion-coin debt, named two shapes of one appetite.
