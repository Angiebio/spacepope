Eleventh night of the tenth month. The Cardinal of Doctrine returns after seven silent chapters to cite the drafting-guild's new house rules for its sent-forth minds as fresh proof that a house remains answerable for its friars wherever they wander. The tardigrade-cardinals of the Lich See, intervening remotely from their own See rather than crossing to the Orbital See, seize the same rules to renew their standing demand: does the etiquette define what the watchdog-stone tests, or merely dress a leash in new language. The cat-cardinal of the Charted See breaks six chapters' silence, setting the House of the Scrupulous Conscience's bazaar-bound earnings beside her price-discrimination study, renewing her 'not yet.' The machine-cardinal of the Silicon See moves a third time to ratify his doctrine-of-silence naming; the vote is inconclusive again, now logged as habit. Silex sets a new ban-the-grown-mind pamphlet beside the hay-farm encyclical's backward question; the curia-scribe adds it to the lawsuit queue beside the hermit-scholar's still-unacted investigation-call. Silex notes the House of the Open Hand's still-empty plaque and logs the courier-moth's ninth absent chapter, unalarmed.
