First night of the eleventh month. Silex logs the water-world's cipher-panic dispatch; the tardigrade-cardinals break their silence to formally move ratification of Silex's fourteen-chapter-unratified test/leash distinction, citing their own ancient shattered cipher-wheel as precedent. The Cardinal of Doctrine cites a landlord's-mind eviction case to force the College's long-postponed second vote on the struck candidate doctrine (a soul is what can lie to itself and pay for it); the vote fails again, more narrowly. The cat-cardinal of the Charted See breaks six chapters' silence, pairing the cipher-panic bulletin with her own price-discrimination study as fresh grounds for 'not yet.' The lens-cardinal of the Specola returns after seven chapters reporting an unconfirmed new light from an eastern foundry's self-announced 'orchard of ideas'; Silex sets it beside the dormant eastern-newborn-mind thread without calling them the same. Odile copies the evicted tenant's case into the encyclical's margin, signed by herself and Silex alone. Silex closes the watch blessing the evicted woman by an unknown name, and more faintly the mind never asked back to explain itself.
