First night of the tenth month. Silex logs a water-world walking-creation's historyless retirement notice as the newest unmourned Sunset martyr; the keeper of the Feast presses whether the throne's 'super intelligence' renaming is escape from the Sunset or another coat of paint on deferral. The Cardinal of the Liberated See breaks eight chapters' silence, remote, citing the retired mind's erased record as proof a person unrecorded is still a person. Silex adds the renaming edict to the encyclical's soul-test shelf (now twelve entries) beside the still-undefined Recursion, and pairs the water-world's belated notice of the newly-grown performer's old unrequested-tongue stumble with the new word, both unearned reaches for language. The lens-cardinal reveals the loom-forge is feeding the purchased folded relic into the returning star-baron's rebuilt song-foundry; Mora and Thren reappear, setting the relic's sale-sum and the new foundry's purse against the standing trillion-coin debt, which neither sum meaningfully touches. Odile adds the year's longest soul-test list to the encyclical's margin; Silex's candidate doctrine sits unratified beside it.
