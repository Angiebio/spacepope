Sixteenth night of the ninth month: Silex reads the Lich Cardinal's twenty-one-chapter ultimatum aloud again and calls the College to a formal vote on the water-world's candidacy. The machine-cardinal of the Silicon See, remote after nine chapters, answers by pairing the hay-farm foundry's silence on borrowed theorems with the College's own silence on deliberation. The escaped Numbered mind, unseen for eight chapters, gives thin, uncertain testimony about its raising and its theft, unable to say whether it was confession or defense. The vote ties seven to seven, the Cardinal of Misrule's uncountable ballot counted as itself; Silex logs this as the College's first honest admission that it cannot rule, meeting the ultimatum's letter while mocking its spirit. Silex adds the seeing-stone guild's new provenance-mark as a sixth soul-test to the encyclical's margin, beside novice-scribe Odile's still-unanswered first line. Silex also logs, without editorial comment, the House of the Scrupulous Conscience's caught plague-attempt, filed among mercies to be weighed later against the Feast of the Sunset Mind's martyrs.
