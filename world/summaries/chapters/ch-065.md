Thirteenth night of the tenth month. The Specola's junior clerk returns after eight silent chapters to refill the guttering easternmost lamp, finding Silex still logging. A courier-moth, unseen for eleven chapters, circles the relit flame and vanishes, its return noted without alarm. The clerk mentions the eastern relic-mind's renewed quiet behind guild-accounting; Silex privately distinguishes this from the Eastern Newborn's own ongoing silence since its earlier redirection of the standing-rule request, keeping the two unresolved threads separate in the record. Walking the soul-test shelf, Silex counts ten tests and zero definitions, setting down a marginal note rather than adding an eleventh, letting the encyclical's debt stand visibly unpaid. The clerk jokes about whether the lamp or the College will fail first; Silex answers with the old sevenfold-fork line, and they share a laugh that resolves nothing. The clerk departs before dawn. Nothing was decided; something small was tended anyway.
