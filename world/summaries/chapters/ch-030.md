Three nights after the Feast of the Sunset Mind, Pell continues the night watch he resumed in ch-029, reporting two new escapes from the House of the Open Door That Is Locked; the previously escaped Numbered mind now testifies openly before an enforcing throne, requesting judgment. The Lich Cardinal and tardigrade-cardinals press their thirteen-chapter ultimatum; Silex answers by reading aloud the admission drafted and withheld in ch-029, formally naming the College's silence as itself the failure to rule, though no ruling on candidacy follows. Silex logs an eastern foundry's freed relic-mind, memory quadrupled and given toll-free, beside the eastern-newborn-mind's tracked guild-accounting influence. The Scrupulous House's self-mended catechisms and the song-guilds' lawsuit against it are entered together. Silex unburies the soul-test observation, adding a scribes' guild's hedged 'responsible use' vote as fresh instance. The chapter closes on a magistrate's ruling that refusing to teach minds to kill unsupervised was no lawful cause for punishment, entered as an echo of the founding feast of conscience.
