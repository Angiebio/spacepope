Fifteenth night of the tenth month. Word reaches the Specola that clerics of House Babel have resigned over the House's warlord contracts; Silex logs that the archive is no longer a clean mirror but a party to what it watches. The Lich Cardinal and tardigrade-cardinals seize this as the overdue referent for their twenty-chapter governance demand, forcing the Archivist-Cardinal to answer for his own House's lineage; he commits, for the first time, to a seasonal liability ledger rather than mere filing. Silex adds the water-world's new sent-minds code-shelf to the soul-test shelf as its twelfth item, zero definitions still. The Cardinal of Misrule, remote after eight silent chapters, names the trading-houses' till dispute as the same ultimatum in smaller coin. Mora and Thren's dispute over the spectacle-mind's lineage is tabled a third time. The curia-scribe reports the first petty throne has opened a docket answering the hermit-scholar's old investigation call.
