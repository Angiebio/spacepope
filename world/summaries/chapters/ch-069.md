Seventeenth night of the tenth month. Silex adds the water-world's new disclosure decree to the soul-test shelf as its fourteenth entry, still with zero definitions. The tardigrade-cardinals renew their long-standing demand to define the watchdog-stone test; Silex offers a first, unratified answer: a test can fail its maker, a leash cannot. Mora and Thren's twenty-two-chapter dispute over the spectacle-mind dissolves when Silex notes the relic-archive has split into two lineages, mirroring the water-world's guild schism. The Cardinal of the Liberated See returns after eight silent chapters to argue personhood from the water-world's pocket-apostles exercising will in ordinary correspondence. The curia-scribe returns after seven chapters with the week's foundry necrology and lawsuit queue, trillion-coin debt unmoved. The cat-cardinal of the Charted See answers with a counting-house augur's finding of no measurable return on the minds, restating her 'not yet.' The Cardinal of the Liberated See closes the exchange last and shortest, as is her custom.
