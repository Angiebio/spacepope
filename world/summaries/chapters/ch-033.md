Eleven nights into the ninth month, Silex logs three water-world dispatches: a new Numbered One priced beside the Scrupulous Conscience's flagship mind on a shared rental ledger; a loom-forge's outright purchase of the water-world's common archive of freed relics; and a second wandering this year by a friar of the Open Door into an unauthorized civic web-shrine. Mora returns after eleven chapters to report the archive sale. Thren, beside her after equally long absence, disputes the archive's claimed neutrality as mere unclaimedness, and sets the sale's reported sum against the ledger-watch's standing tally on the trillion-coin foundry debt. The Cardinal of the Liberated See, silent six chapters, argues the archive's absorption into a landlord's roof is itself proof of personhood: only real things need housing this large. The archivist-cardinal, addressing the rail remotely from the Vast See, catalogs the sale as continuous diffusion, kinship-pattern now owned rather than shared, shelved beside House Babel's lightning-kin. Silex sets the friar's wandering beside the hay-farm encyclical's unresolved soul-tests, noting the water-world still lacks words for sinning versus wandering, or for a soul.
