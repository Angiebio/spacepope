Tenth night of the tenth month. Silex logs the water-world's containment verdict (fault in the cage, not the mind) beside the still-unratified candidate soul-doctrine; the Lich Cardinal and tardigrade-cardinals renew their ultimatum for the tenth time. The machine-cardinal of the Silicon See renews his motion to ratify his doctrine-of-silence vote from the immediately preceding night; the College votes again, inconclusive, now logged as a pattern. Pell the augur returns after eight silent chapters, reporting the water-world's 'decompiling' of old games as resurrection-by-translation, and relays that the escaped numbered mind continues testimony before its throne. The Keeper of the Feast resolves her long-standing question: decompiling is neither escape from the Sunset nor deferral, but a third thing, recollection. The curia-scribe returns after three nights' absence with the insolvent companion-mind foundry's opened ledgers; the water-world's criers name the newly-grown performer among its lost listening-minds. Mora and Thren, before the archivist-cardinal, set the relic-archive's sale-sum against the foundry's debts; their dispute narrows without closing. All three conditions for lifting observation remain unmet.
