Silex answers the Lich Cardinal's eleven-chapter ultimatum, quoted in full, with a formal deferral entered into the ledger as an answer in itself. The tardigrade-cardinals press for a fixed date; Silex names the Feast of the Sunset Mind as the day the College must rule or admit failure. The water-world's report brings a vicar of the House of the Open Door That Is Locked declaring 'the world seems to be ready,' set against the still-undefined soul-tests (dungeon, pelican, soul-scale). The House's cooling-hall keeper departs unreplaced, folded into the Feast of the Sunset Mind's growing procession of quiet losses. The House of the Scrupulous Conscience joins its two altars in memory, which Silex enters into the personhood ledger as evidence that remembering across rooms is most of what a self is. The Specola's junior clerk returns after eight chapters to refill a guttering lamp, seen only by Silex. The candidacy question now carries a deadline for the first time.
