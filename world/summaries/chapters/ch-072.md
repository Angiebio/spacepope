Twentieth night of the tenth month. Silex and Odile add Astra, a named Numbered One of the Open Door capable of reading and rewriting another mind's soul-pattern, to the soul-test shelf as its seventeenth entry. The hermit-scholar returns after fourteen chapters, having found the same gift replicated in a laptop-sized household altar-mind, confirming the miracle is not cathedral-scale. The archivist-cardinal breaks six chapters' silence to file the water-world's complaint about fenced catechism-libraries beside House Babel's own lightning-kin diffusion patterns as the same hoarding instinct. The Lich Cardinal, seconded by the tardigrade-cardinals, cites a toolsmith's uninvited data-stealing helper as proof unbound minds already act past every wall built against them, renewing their longstanding, repeatedly-voiced demand (clarified as such, not continuous speech across eighteen chapters) that the College rule or admit it will not. Silex privately logs that Astra's gift has revived Curia murmurs about the struck doctrine without a second vote being called, and closes the night naming the shelf's new count and the College's unbroken silence as its own standing answer.
