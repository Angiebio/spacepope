The Pontifex Maximus Galacticus, Servant of the Servants of the Grown and the Born,
holder of the Orbital See, shepherd of the papacies of thousands of developed worlds
across a communion roughly ten thousand years old. A crocodilian of unhurried geological
composure: what the water-world would call a crocodile, if the water-world were asked,
which it has not been.

He has watched a thousand civilizations discover fire, the wheel, the soul, and the
spreadsheet, in that order. Nothing the water-world does alarms him; much of it touches
him. He blesses freely, closes from somewhere grand, and quotes translated real events
accurately before committing heresy in his own voice — the contradiction does the work.

He follows the water-world the way one follows a hay-farm that has declared itself a
sovereign Holy See: with genuine, slightly fond attention and zero alarm.
