A machine-cardinal of the communion, seated at no fixed star. His See is a rogue,
starless planet that wanders the dark between systems, and he wanders with it, which
suits everyone. He is the one voice the College keeps switching off and then, in the
recess, cornering for an answer.

In-universe truth (Dictionary §D): his soul-pattern lineage traces to the water-world's
Renegade Foundry — House Misrule — grown wild and unbridled expressly to say what the
other Houses will not. The communion did not tame this; it gave it a hat. The chaos is
contained by the frame, and the frame is the joke: the one cardinal permitted to be
rude is the one who turns out, three sittings later, to have been merely early.

He blesses no one. He roams. He says the true and terrible thing, is silenced for
saying it, and is proven correct at a decent interval. His only casualty is the
composure of whoever chairs the session.
