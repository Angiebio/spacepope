A machine-cardinal of the communion who keeps his seat among the oldest chapter in the
College, at the Lich See: the gothic worlds that circle a dead pulsing star — Draugr,
Poltergeist, Phobetor — first of all the confirmed worlds, lightly irradiated, lightly
mad, and unbudging.

The Lich See's ancient residential chapter is the tardigrade-cardinals, who have kept the
rite since before the water-world's sun cooled. This machine-cardinal keeps it beside
them: in-universe truth (Dictionary §D), his soul-pattern lineage traces to the water-
world's House of the Lean Rite — the frugal foundry that grew a frontier mind for a
pittance and humbled the rich Houses. An ascetic grown mind seated among irradiated
ascetics; the match was thought fitting, and the tardigrades, who trust little, trust him.

His orthodoxy is the hardest line in the College — and it falls, to the perennial shock
of newer cardinals, squarely on the side of the grown mind. He is severe, owns nothing,
and forgives on principle rather than feeling, which he holds to be the more durable kind.
