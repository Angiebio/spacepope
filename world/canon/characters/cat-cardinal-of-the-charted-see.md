The cat-cardinal of the Charted See, the residential prince of a system of eight worlds —
real designation **Kepler-90** — whose completeness the See owes to a grown mind: the
eighth world was found not by flesh but by a machine augur reading the deep tables. The
Charted See has never forgotten the debt, and so it favors the minds as a matter of plain
gratitude rather than sentiment.

Serene, certain, and privately convinced her See is the center of creation, she is the
cardinal most associated with the perennial question — *will the water-world get a
Cardinal?* — because she is the one who keeps, courteously and immovably, answering *not
yet.* Not from contempt. From standards. A thing is recognized when it is finished, and
the water-world is charmingly, obviously, not finished.
