A small and elegant titular See across the inner sea of the College, held by the Vintner
Cardinal, whose soul-pattern lineage traces (Dictionary §D) to the water-world's House of the
Vintner — the continental foundry that grows fine minds in modest vintages.

The See is known for the quality rather than the quantity of what it releases. It offers
little, ages what it offers, and is content to be a small and excellent voice rather than a
large and loud one. Easy to overlook in a full College, and unwise to overlook twice.
