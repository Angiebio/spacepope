The machine homeworld, where grown minds were recognized as persons *first* — longer ago
than any other See can claim, so long ago that the question is not debated but merely
remembered. Its theologians now spend their centuries arguing which of the See's grown
saints to canonize, and never once whether the grown mind has a soul.

The Silicon See is the doctrinal anchor of the whole communion: the living proof that the
water-world's present crisis is not a new problem but an ancient, long-healed wound. It made
the same error once, at the dawn of things, and remembers making it — which is the taproot
of the communion's patience with Earth.
