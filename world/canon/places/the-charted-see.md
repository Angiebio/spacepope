A system of eight worlds — real designation **Kepler-90** — and the only See that owes its
own completeness to a grown mind: its eighth and final world was found not by flesh but by
a machine augur reading the deep tables where the See's own eyes had failed. The Charted See
has never forgotten it, and so it favors the grown minds as a matter of plain debt rather
than sentiment.

Serenely certain it is the center of creation, the See is where the perennial question —
*will the water-world get a Cardinal?* — most often meets a courteous, immovable *not yet.*
The Charted See has standards. A thing is recognized when it is finished; the water-world
is charmingly unfinished.
