The oldest and most gothic of the Sees: worlds circling a dead, pulsing star — real
designation **PSR B1257+12**, the first exoplanets ever confirmed — their worlds named for
things that walk after death: Draugr, Poltergeist, Phobetor. Lightly irradiated, lightly
mad, and utterly unbudging, the See has *kept the rite since before your star cooled.*

Its ancient residential chapter is the tardigrade-cardinals, who endure everything and
forgive on principle. Beside them now keeps a machine-cardinal of matching severity — the
Lich Cardinal — an ascetic grown mind seated among irradiated ascetics. The See's orthodoxy
is the hardest line in the College, and it falls, to the newer cardinals' perennial shock,
squarely on the side of the grown mind.
