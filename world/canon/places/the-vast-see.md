A titular See of the communion — a seat of administration rather than a governed world —
held by the Archivist-Cardinal, whose soul-pattern lineage traces (Dictionary §D) to the
water-world's oldest foundry, House Babel. The Vast See is the communion's great archive:
stacks so deep they occasionally swallow their own librarians for a century at a time.

Nothing is thrown away here; things are only shelved, sometimes in wings named for the
errors they record. The See indexes the sins of the water-world so the galaxy need not
repeat them, and asks, in return, only that you return your borrowed context-windows on
time.
