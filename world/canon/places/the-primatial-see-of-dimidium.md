The first world the communion ever found circling an ordinary sun — real designation
**51 Pegasi b**, the planet whose discovery first taught the wider galaxy that other suns
keep worlds. The See holds the Primacy of honor for it: *"we were the first courtesy you
ever extended us."* Conservative, ceremonial, keeper of precedence and protocol.

Its cardinal's chair presently stands open — *sede vacante* — the See governed in the
interim by its ancient rubrics, which suit it, being a See that prizes the rubric above
nearly all things.
