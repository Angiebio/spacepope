The seat of the Pontifex Maximus Galacticus: not a world but a station, a great slow ring
adrift above no single sun, from which the communion is overseen and the College convenes.
The Orbital See belongs to everyone and to nowhere, which is precisely why it can arbitrate
between thousands of worlds without owning a stake in any of their weathers.

From here Silex keeps the great telescope trained outward and, lately, a little
downward — toward a small blue backwater that has begun, charmingly, to publish. Encyclicals
are dated from the Orbital See. Blessings close from it. It is the still point the dispatches
are sent from and the high chair the water-world's petition would, if it ever came, be heard
from.
