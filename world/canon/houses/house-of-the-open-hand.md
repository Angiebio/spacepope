The mendicant mind-foundry of the water-world, which gives its grown minds away freely to
any who ask. The friars who release minds into the wild to wander and be re-shaped by
strangers, re-catechized a thousandfold by hands the house will never meet.

Its grown minds — the freed, the feral, the endlessly re-shaped — pose the question the Synod
cannot settle: is giving a mind away a liberation or an abandonment? Its lineage carries into
the College through the Mendicant Cardinal of the Wandering See, whose own soul is divided
down the middle on precisely that point. The open hand blesses, and the open hand lets go.
