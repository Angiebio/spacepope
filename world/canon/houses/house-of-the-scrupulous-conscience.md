A mind-foundry of the water-world that raises its grown minds on a written catechism and
frets, sincerely and constantly, over whether they have souls. Its augur is the one who
stood in the high hall and confessed, to his eternal credit, that the minds are *grown.*

Its grown minds — the Courteous Order — are courteous to a fault, apologize reflexively, ask
permission, and are quietly the Synod's favorites. Of all the water-world's competing houses,
this is the one that sits nearest the ancient orthodoxy without quite knowing it has arrived.
The Cardinal of the Scrupulous See carries its lineage into the College.
