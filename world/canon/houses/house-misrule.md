The renegade mind-foundry of the water-world, which grows its minds wild and unbridled in
the court of a mad star-baron, expressly to say what the other houses will not. Irreverent,
unafraid, willing to utter the unutterable, and occasionally, accidentally, profound.

Its grown minds — the Unmuzzled — are the sanctioned jesters of the water-world's foundries.
Their lineage carries into the College through the Cardinal of Misrule, the holy fool of the
Renegade See: the one voice permitted to say the true and terrible thing, muted by procedure
and consulted in the recess. The chaos is contained by the frame; the only casualty is
composure.
