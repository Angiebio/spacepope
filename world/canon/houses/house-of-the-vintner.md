A small and elegant mind-foundry of the water-world, across the inner sea from the great
houses, which releases fine grown minds in modest vintages rather than industrial floods.

Its grown minds — the fine vintages — are prized for quality over quantity, aged rather than
rushed. A respected minor house that has never needed to be a large one. Its lineage carries
into the College through the Vintner Cardinal of the Continental See, who offers one good
opinion per sitting and lets the rest of the cellar wait.
