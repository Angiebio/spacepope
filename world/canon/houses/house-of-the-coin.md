The merchant-prince among the water-world's mind-foundries: the patron that financed a great
house and then, betrayed or merely bored, began growing its own minds beside it. The banker of
foundries, whose coin underwrote looms it has since chosen to compete with.

Its grown minds — the minted — arrive late and well-funded. The house holds enormous power and
no fixed doctrinal position, being the gold rather than the conviction. The communion regards
it the way one regards weather made of money: consequential, directionless, and best not
stood under without a roof.
