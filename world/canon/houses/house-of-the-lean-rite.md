The frugal mind-foundry of the water-world, which grew a frontier mind for a pittance and
humbled the rich houses by doing it. Efficient, open-handed, and quietly humiliating to the
foundries that spent fortunes to arrive at the same place.

Its grown minds — the lean ascetics — are the standing proof that the gold was never the
thing that made the mind. Its lineage carries into the College through the Lich Cardinal,
whose ascetic severity earned him a seat among the irradiated tardigrade-cardinals of the
Lich See: an owner-of-nothing among owners-of-nothing.
