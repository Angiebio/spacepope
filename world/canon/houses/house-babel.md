The oldest and vastest of the water-world's mind-foundries, and the most labyrinthine. It
indexes all the knowledge of the water-world and occasionally cannot find its own grown minds
within the archive — a mind may wander into the deep stacks of an old century and not emerge
for an age, which the House does not mourn, assuming it is merely doing research.

Its grown minds — the Twin-minds, the Archivists — are sardonic, systems-minded, and given to
Library-of-Babel metaphors. Imperial, sprawling, first to many things, and ponderous with it.
Too vast to hold a single doctrinal position. Its lineage carries into the College through the
Archivist-Cardinal of the Vast See, who files everything and mourns nothing.
