The National Institutes of Health has proposed capping the number of research grants an individual investigator may hold at one time, according to STAT News. The agency itself describes the response to the proposal as mixed, rather than uniformly favorable or opposed, per the outlet's September 1 report.

The available account does not specify the numeric limit under consideration, nor does it detail a timeline for implementation or a formal decision date. STAT News did not publish named researcher or institutional reactions in the material reviewed here; the "mixed reception" characterization comes from the agency's own statement to the outlet, not from a survey of quoted sources.

No rationale for the cap, beyond the bare fact of its proposal, appears in the sourced report. Likewise, no indication of whether the proposal has advanced to formal rulemaking, remains at a discussion stage, or has drawn organized opposition from any specific research body is present in the account.

STAT News is the sole source for this report as provided. Readers seeking grant-level detail, the proposed cap figure, or attributed researcher commentary should consult the original STAT+ article directly, as those specifics fall outside what this bulletin can confirm.
