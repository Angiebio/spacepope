Bulletin: CHAI Working Group on Frontier Model Security

The Coalition for Health AI (CHAI) has formed a new work group to address cybersecurity risks tied to frontier AI models, according to MedTech Dive. The initiative aims to produce playbooks that help healthcare organizations both defend against threats posed by increasingly capable AI systems and make use of these systems' capabilities in their own security practices.

MedTech Dive's report frames the effort as a response to the dual-edged nature of frontier models within healthcare settings, systems powerful enough to introduce new vulnerabilities even as they offer new defensive tools. The work group's stated purpose, per the available summary, is to translate that tension into practical guidance rather than abstract policy.

No further detail on the work group's membership, timeline, or specific deliverables was included in the available source material. The report does not specify which organizations or individuals will lead the effort, nor does it indicate when the first playbooks might be published.

CHAI has positioned itself in prior reporting as a body seeking to establish shared standards for AI deployment in health systems. This latest work group extends that mandate into the security domain specifically, treating cyber risk as a distinct category requiring its own guidance rather than a subset of general AI governance.

Further specifics await future reporting.
