STAT News reports, in the November 18 edition of its AI Prognosis newsletter, that the market for AI-powered medical scribes is beginning to sort into discernible winners and losers. The outlet frames this shakeout as the newsletter's central subject for the week, alongside dispatches from the HLTH conference, an industry gathering where health technology vendors and health systems compare notes on deployment.

According to STAT News, the pattern of adoption and attrition among scribe vendors matters because it signals which artificial intelligence products are earning durable trust from clinicians inside actual clinical workflows, not merely in pilot programs or vendor demonstrations. The newsletter treats this trust, once established, as a leading indicator for how broadly AI tools will be adopted across other corners of healthcare delivery.

The dispatch is presented as one segment of a longer roundup; STAT News indicates there is additional HLTH-related reporting and other items bundled in the same edition, though the provided material does not specify which vendors are gaining ground, which are faltering, or what evidence underlies the newsletter's assessment. The piece is published behind STAT's STAT+ subscription tier.

No further operational detail, financial figures, or named company outcomes are given in the material supplied.
