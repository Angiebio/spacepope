The U.S. Food and Drug Administration announced on October 14 the launch of the Rare Disease Framework Initiative. According to the agency's press announcement, the initiative aims to develop what the FDA calls a holistic, fit-for-purpose framework for rare disease drug development.

The FDA is a division within the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. The announcement states the initiative is intended to reimagine how drugs and biologics for rare diseases are developed, though the agency's release, as summarized, does not detail specific mechanisms, timelines, or regulatory changes beyond this stated intent.

No further particulars, such as the scope of diseases covered, funding, or implementation steps, are provided in the available material. The announcement does not specify whether the initiative involves new legislation, internal FDA policy guidance, or coordination with outside bodies.
