Endpoints News reports that Sanofi has agreed to acquire Insmed for $41 billion, the largest biopharmaceutical acquisition since 2023 and, per the outlet, Sanofi's strongest wager since 2004. The deal was announced Tuesday.

According to the report, Insmed's path to this acquisition spans 38 years, a company history the outlet characterizes as a "complicated saga." Endpoints News frames the transaction as the culmination of that long arc rather than a sudden corporate maneuver.

The source summary identifies Insmed as a respiratory drugmaker, and notes the acquisition's scale places it among the sector's largest recent transactions. No further financial terms, closing conditions, or executive statements are included in the available material.

The provided summary also states that the deal reshapes the landscape for respiratory and rare disease therapies, with implications described as extending to the development of what the material calls "mind-adjacent treatments."
