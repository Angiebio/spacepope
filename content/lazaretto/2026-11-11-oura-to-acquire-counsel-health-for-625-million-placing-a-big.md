Oura, the smart ring manufacturer, announced it will acquire Counsel Health, a clinical AI startup, for $625 million, according to STAT News. The deal comes as Oura has recently delayed its plans to go public.

STAT News reports the acquisition represents a significant wager by Oura on artificial intelligence as a component of direct patient care, extending the company's business beyond its core wearable ring hardware into clinical software territory.

Details of the transaction's structure, timeline, and the specific terms governing Counsel Health's integration into Oura's operations were not fully specified in available reporting. The size of the deal, at $625 million, places it among the more substantial acquisitions in the wearable health technology sector this year.

The move follows a broader pattern in the industry of device makers seeking to pair biometric data collection with AI-driven clinical interpretation and guidance. Oura's ring products measure sleep, readiness, and other physiological signals; Counsel Health's clinical AI capabilities would reportedly extend the company's offerings toward direct health guidance functions.

STAT News did not report immediate reaction from regulators or competitors to the announcement. The acquisition's effect on Oura's previously delayed public offering plans was not addressed in the available account. Further details on integration timelines and product plans were not disclosed in the initial reporting.
