Roche has entered a drug discovery agreement with Defand Therapeutics, a Chinese biotechnology company, according to STAT News. The collaboration centers on molecular glue medicines, a class of drugs designed to force proximity between proteins that would not otherwise interact, with the aim of triggering the degradation of disease-causing targets.

STAT News reported the deal on October 8, confirming the two companies as parties to the agreement and identifying its focus as molecular glue drug discovery.
