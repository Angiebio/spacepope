Tango Therapeutics reported Thursday that an experimental combination therapy produced tumor responses in 82% of patients with a certain type of advanced pancreatic cancer, according to STAT News, which covered the data presented at ESMO 2026.

The combination pairs Tango's vopimetostat with Revolution Medicines' rasonque, STAT reported. Pancreatic cancer remains among the most difficult malignancies to treat, with few targeted options showing strong activity in advanced disease, and STAT's coverage situates the reported response rate against that backdrop of limited existing options.

STAT's report, published under its STAT+ subscription tier, did not specify in the material provided the trial's full patient count, dosing details, or duration of response, details that would typically accompany a conference presentation of this kind. The bulletin's source summary attributes the 82% figure directly to Tango's own reporting of the trial results.
