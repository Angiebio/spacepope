The White House on July 24 released a blueprint outlining its priorities for U.S. science policy, according to STAT News. The document calls for redirecting federal research funding away from universities and toward industry, with STAT reporting the shift could affect hundreds of billions of dollars over time.

The blueprint places heavy emphasis on artificial intelligence as a national research priority, while proposing reduced federal support for life sciences relative to current levels. STAT's coverage frames this as a departure from the postwar model in which federal grants flowed primarily to academic institutions, which then conducted basic research on the government's behalf.

The report is described as having drawn a mixed reaction, though the specific reactions of named scientists, agencies, or university groups were not detailed in the available summary. STAT's reporting indicates the plan represents an administration-wide statement of intent rather than an enacted policy, meaning implementation would require further budgetary and legislative action.

No additional figures, agency names, or specific dollar amounts beyond the "hundreds of billions" estimate were provided in the source material.
