Hardin writes that artificial intelligence "is not the villain here, it is a mirror." The essay frames AI's arrival in medicine as reflecting, rather than causing, a diminishment of physician judgment already present in the profession.

The piece appears under STAT's opinion section, addressing how AI tools are being positioned in discussions of clinical practice.
