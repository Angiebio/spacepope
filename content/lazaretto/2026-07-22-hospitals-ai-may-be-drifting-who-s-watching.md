Three authors writing in STAT News argue that hospital leaders are not adequately overseeing the artificial intelligence systems already running inside their institutions. Peter Pronovost, Justin Norden, and Kedar Mate write that oversight of these tools has too often been left to a technology committee that meets on a quarterly schedule, rather than functioning as a continuous responsibility of senior leadership.

The authors' central contention is that AI governance in hospitals should be treated as an ongoing duty of the people at the top, not a periodic item on a committee agenda. Their piece is framed as an opinion contribution addressing how health systems manage the AI tools they have put into use.
