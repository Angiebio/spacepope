According to the outlet's account, the conversation arose informally among guests of the event, described as taking place over wine, and centered on how far artificial intelligence tools might go in automating the functions a small biotech company currently requires staff to perform.

The piece, published as part of Endpoints News's "Post-Hoc Live" series, frames the idea as one raised among industry guests rather than as an established prediction, and the outlet characterizes it as among the "out-there ideas" that emerged from the dinner conversation.
