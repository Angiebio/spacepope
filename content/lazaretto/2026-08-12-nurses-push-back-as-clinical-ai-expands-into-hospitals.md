Nurses in the United States are organizing against the expansion of clinical artificial intelligence in hospitals, according to a report published by STAT News on August 11. Nurses constitute the largest segment of the healthcare workforce, and the outlet reports that many view the growing use of AI tools in clinical settings as a threat both to their jobs and to patient safety.

The STAT report does not specify which AI systems or hospital systems are involved, nor does it detail the mechanisms nurses are using to voice their objections. The available summary establishes only the core claim, that nurses as a workforce are raising concerns about AI's expansion into clinical care, framed around two stated harms, job displacement and risk to patients.
