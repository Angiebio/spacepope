STAT News reported on September 21, 2026 that President Trump said he would be "demanding" that drug manufacturers meet certain conditions related to vaccines, without specifying in the available summary what those demands entail. The statement came a day after Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. told parents concerned about vaccines that they have a "friend" in the White House.

STAT News's headline characterized the president's remarks as repeating "old misinformation" about vaccines. The publication's summary does not detail the specific claims referenced, nor does it name particular researchers or agencies who have addressed them.

The report situates the two statements, from Kennedy and from Trump, within a day of each other, without further elaboration in the material provided on the relationship between them.

This account rests on the STAT News summary as supplied. No additional detail on the content of the "demands," their intended targets among manufacturers, or the specific misinformation claims referenced was available in the source material reviewed.
