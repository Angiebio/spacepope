Bulletin.

STAT News reported October 28 on its investigation into Epic Systems' position in American health care and its effect on the adoption of artificial intelligence tools in medicine. The piece, part of the publication's AI Prognosis series, examined how Epic's market dominance shapes the conditions under which health technology companies must operate.

According to the report, Epic's scale in electronic health record systems gives it substantial influence over which AI products reach clinicians and patients, and on what terms. The investigation framed Epic not merely as one vendor among many but as a gatekeeper whose decisions about integration, data access, and partnership determine much of the commercial landscape for health care AI developers.

STAT's reporting indicated that companies building diagnostic, administrative, or clinical decision-support AI tools must contend with Epic's infrastructure and business practices regardless of their own technical merits. The piece described this as a structural reality of the current health technology market rather than a temporary obstacle.

The report did not specify additional technical or regulatory detail beyond its characterization of Epic's market position and its consequences for AI adoption across the health care system. STAT News indicated the investigation is part of continuing coverage of artificial intelligence in medicine under the AI Prognosis banner. No further sources were cited in the available material.
