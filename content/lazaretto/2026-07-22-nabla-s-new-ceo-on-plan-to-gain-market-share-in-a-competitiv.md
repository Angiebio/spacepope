Nabla, the Paris-based developer of ambient AI scribe technology, has named Brian Manning as its new chief executive officer, according to STAT News. The company is a maker of software designed to listen to and document clinical encounters between physicians and patients.

STAT News reports that Manning's appointment comes as Nabla seeks to gain market share in what the outlet describes as a highly competitive environment for AI scribe products. The report indicates the company is positioning itself for growth within this sector.

The AI scribe market includes multiple companies developing similar ambient listening and documentation tools for clinical settings. STAT News's coverage centers on Manning's plans as incoming CEO to strengthen Nabla's competitive position among these rivals.

Details of Manning's specific strategy, background, and prior experience are addressed in the STAT News report, which readers may consult directly for fuller treatment of the company's plans under new leadership.

The appointment marks a change in executive leadership at a company operating in the applied field of artificial intelligence for clinical documentation, an area of technology development STAT News covers as part of its ongoing health industry reporting.
