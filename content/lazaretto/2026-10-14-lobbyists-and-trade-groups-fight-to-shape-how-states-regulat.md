STAT News reported on October 14, 2026, that lobbying groups and trade associations are working behind the scenes to shape how states regulate artificial intelligence in medicine. According to the report, groups representing doctors and hospitals are among those involved in these efforts.

The report, published under STAT's subscription STAT+ service, frames the activity as a contest over influence, with multiple organized interests seeking to shape the direction of state-level rules governing medical AI. STAT's account does not specify the particular rules under discussion or which states are involved, nor does it detail the substantive positions taken by the groups named.

STAT characterizes the matter as significant for how artificial intelligence systems will ultimately be deployed within healthcare settings, though the report as summarized does not elaborate on the mechanisms by which that deployment would be affected.

The full report is available to STAT+ subscribers.
