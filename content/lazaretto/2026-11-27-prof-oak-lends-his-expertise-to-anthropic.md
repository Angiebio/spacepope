Endpoints News reports that Cody Tranbarger has become involved in biology research and development at Anthropic, the artificial intelligence company. According to the outlet, Tranbarger revealed his identity last month after operating for a period as a prolific pseudonymous poster on X, a persona referenced in Endpoints' headline by the nickname "Prof Oak." The outlet frames his arrival as part of Anthropic's broader adjustment following its IPO, a milestone the company recently completed.

Endpoints describes the development as notable chiefly for its unlikeliness, a biology-focused figure known previously through an online persona now drawn into the operations of one of the leading AI firms. The report does not specify Tranbarger's formal title or the scope of his duties beyond describing him as lending expertise to the company's biology efforts.

The outlet situates this appointment within a larger pattern it is tracking, in which Anthropic's post-IPO footing appears to be drawing new kinds of talent, including figures whose public profiles originated outside conventional corporate or academic biology circles. Endpoints does not elaborate on the financial terms of Tranbarger's role or on how his X-based reputation translated into the position.
