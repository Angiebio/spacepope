The Federal Trade Commission has filed suit against Hims & Hers, the direct-to-consumer telehealth company, alleging the firm misled consumers through deceptive business practices and violated their privacy, according to a report from STAT News published July 29.

The STAT News account, headlined "Hims misled consumers and violated their privacy, FTC alleges in a lawsuit," indicates the federal agency's complaint centers on how the company represented itself and handled consumer data in the course of its telehealth operations. Hims & Hers has built a substantial business connecting consumers directly with prescribers for conditions ranging from hair loss to sexual health to weight management, a model that depends heavily on marketing claims and the collection of sensitive health information.

The filing places a prominent player in the direct-to-consumer health sector under formal federal scrutiny. STAT News frames the action as part of a broader pattern of regulatory attention directed at telehealth companies whose marketing and data practices have drawn questions from consumers and watchdogs alike.

Further details of the FTC's specific claims, the requested remedies, and any response from Hims & Hers were not included in the available reporting. The Specola will note further coverage as the litigation proceeds through federal court.
