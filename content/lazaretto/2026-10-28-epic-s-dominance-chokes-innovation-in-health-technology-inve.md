A STAT News investigation published October 28 reports that Epic Systems selectively withholds access to patient data, a practice that disadvantages rival software developers and stymies innovation in health technology, according to the outlet's findings.

The investigation characterizes Epic's position in the electronic health record market as dominant, with the company's control over data access described as an obstacle for competitors seeking to build alternative tools. STAT's reporting centers on the mechanics of this withholding and its effects on the broader health technology landscape, framing the company's practices as a chokepoint for innovation across the sector.

The report does not detail the specific technical means by which Epic restricts access, nor does it specify which companies or products have been most affected. STAT's findings, as summarized, rest on the central claim that Epic's selective control over patient data has a chilling effect on competitors attempting to develop and offer alternatives within the health technology space.

No additional context on regulatory response, industry reaction, or Epic's own statements accompanies this summary. The investigation's scope, per the available material, is limited to the data-access practices themselves and their reported consequence for innovation, without further elaboration on downstream effects or responses from other parties.
