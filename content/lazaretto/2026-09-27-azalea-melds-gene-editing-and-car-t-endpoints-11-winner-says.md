Endpoints News named Azalea the winner of its Endpoints 11 2026 competition, reporting that the company combines gene editing with CAR-T cell therapy techniques.

According to the outlet, Azalea's approach merges two previously distinct biotechnology platforms: the precise genomic alteration methods associated with gene editing, and the engineered immune-cell strategies developed for CAR-T therapies, which modify a patient's own T-cells to target disease. Endpoints News reported this combination as the basis for the company's recognition in the competition, which the outlet uses to highlight biotech firms it considers noteworthy within a given year's field.

The report did not specify further technical details of Azalea's platform, its clinical pipeline, its funding history, or the composition of its leadership beyond identifying it as this year's Endpoints 11 selection. No additional named sources, executives, or investors were cited in the material provided.

The published account frames the achievement primarily as a milestone of recognition rather than of clinical results, noting the selection itself as the news event. Endpoints News dated its report September 25, 2026.

The convergence of gene editing and cellular immunotherapy described here reflects a broader industry pattern of combining programmable biological tools, an area of engineering interest extending beyond therapeutic applications into the design of adaptable biological systems more generally.
