Simon Willison, writing on his blog on September 25, 2026, quoted John Gruber's commentary on Muse, a new agentic AI product. According to the quoted passage, originally published at Daring Fireball, Muse has drawn significant attention, including Gruber's own, for combining technical novelty with ease of use.

The technical novelty, per the quoted text, is that each user receives their own persistent Linux virtual machine running in Meta's cloud. Gruber's remarks, as quoted by Willison, note that this architecture is paired with packaging designed to be easy to install and easy to use for ordinary consumers.

The quoted material also describes how Muse is presented to users. Gruber's text states that the product is packaged with a mascot, describing it as "a cute mascot," and links to earlier commentary discussing that presentation choice, dated September 24, 2026.

Willison's post frames Muse, per the source material, as a notable entry in the category of consumer-accessible agentic AI. The accompanying summary material describes Muse's persistent-VM approach as significant because it could shape how personal AI agents are architected going forward, giving individual users continuous, stateful computing environments tied to their AI assistant rather than a stateless session.

The bulletin's underlying sources consist of Willison's September 25, 2026 post and Gruber's original Daring Fireball commentary, dated the same period, both cited via the linked URLs in the story material provided.
