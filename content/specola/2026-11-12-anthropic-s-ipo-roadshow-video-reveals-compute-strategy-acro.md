Anthropic began its IPO roadshow on November 10, 2026, releasing a video now posted at ipo.anthropic.com that discloses previously unreleased details about the company's operations. The material was reported by Simon Willison on November 11.

According to Willison's account, the roadshow video includes a segment titled "Approach to compute," featuring Anthropic co-founder Tom Brown. In this section, Brown describes how Anthropic trains and serves its Claude models across four distinct hardware platforms: AWS Trainium chips, Google TPUs, NVIDIA GPUs, and AMD GPUs.

Willison characterizes this disclosure as particularly interesting, noting that the multi-vendor compute strategy had not been laid out in this form before. The roadshow video is part of the standard IPO process, in which companies present business information to prospective investors ahead of a public offering.

No further specifics from the video's content, such as the proportion of workloads assigned to each hardware type or the reasoning behind the diversification, were included in the available summary. The source material identifies the video as hosted on Anthropic's official IPO website, under a section labeled "roadshow-video."

The disclosure offers one of the more concrete public glimpses into how a major AI developer structures its underlying computing infrastructure, information not typically included in standard product announcements or research publications.
