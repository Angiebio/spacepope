Anthropic has filed officially for an initial public offering, according to TechCrunch AI. The filing, reported October 26, discloses financial and structural details of the company's business for the first time in public form.

TechCrunch's coverage describes soaring revenue alongside mounting losses, a combination the filing attributes to rapid scaling of operations. The report also notes major enterprise adoption, indicating that corporate customers account for a significant share of Anthropic's business activity, though the summary provided does not specify figures.

The filing further outlines what TechCrunch characterizes as an unusual governance structure. This structure is reportedly designed to preserve founder control over the company even as it moves toward public markets, while also maintaining the company's stated commitment to AI safety as an organizing mission. The filing apparently frames these governance provisions as a mechanism for balancing shareholder interests against the company's founding mandate.

No additional operational or infrastructure details are included in the source material provided. TechCrunch's report, as summarized, focuses on the financial disclosures, the adoption figures, and the governance arrangement as the filing's notable elements.

The filing marks a significant step for Anthropic, which has operated as a major developer of large language model systems. Further details of the IPO timeline, including pricing and listing venue, were not included in the available coverage.
