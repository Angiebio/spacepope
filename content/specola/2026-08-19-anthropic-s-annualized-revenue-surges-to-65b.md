Anthropic's annualized revenue has reached $65 billion, TechCrunch AI reported August 17, up from $47 billion two months earlier, an increase of $18 billion in that span.

The figure is annualized, a projection derived from current revenue run rate rather than audited annual earnings. TechCrunch AI's report did not specify the underlying monthly or quarterly figures used to calculate the projection, nor did it detail the customer segments or product lines driving the increase.

The outlet's framing places the growth within the broader economics of foundation model companies, noting implications for what the report calls the "foundry ecosystem," the network of firms building and deploying large language models. No further detail on that framing was given in the available material.

No comment from Anthropic is quoted in the source. No comparison figures from competing model makers were provided. The report does not state whether the $18 billion increase reflects new enterprise contracts, expanded usage by existing customers, pricing changes, or some combination of factors.

This bulletin summarizes the single cited report. Independent verification of the $65 billion figure, and of the two-month growth window, was not available in the source material provided to this desk.
