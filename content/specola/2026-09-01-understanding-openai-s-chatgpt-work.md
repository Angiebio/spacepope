OpenAI announced ChatGPT Work on July 9th, according to the company's own posted description of the product as a tool "for your most ambitious work." Simon Willison, writing on his weblog on August 30th, reports that OpenAI has continued revising the product steadily since that launch. He describes ChatGPT Work as "an extraordinarily confusing and very powerful product" and says his post sets out what he has determined about it so far, based on his own use and observation.

Willison's central finding is structural. He writes that ChatGPT Work is actually two separate products bundled under one name. The version he considers more significant runs in the cloud rather than on a user's own machine, and can be reached through the chatgpt.com web interface.

The material available does not extend past this point of the breakdown. Willison does not, in the portion summarized here, detail the second of the two products, nor does he specify pricing, availability, or the technical architecture underlying the cloud-based mode. No statement from OpenAI beyond the July 9th announcement is quoted or paraphrased in this account, and no further OpenAI commentary on the July-to-August changes is cited. The record, as it stands, establishes the product's dual nature and its cloud access point, and nothing more.
