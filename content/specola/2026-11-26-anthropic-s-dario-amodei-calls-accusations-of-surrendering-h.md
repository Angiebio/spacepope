Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei has publicly rejected accusations from rivals that he favors ceding human control over artificial intelligence systems, calling such characterizations "absurd," according to TechCrunch. The exchange comes amid a broader industry trend in which leading AI companies are increasingly automating their own research and model development processes.

TechCrunch reports that Amodei's pushback addresses claims circulating among competitors in the AI sector that he and Anthropic support diminishing human agency in favor of AI autonomy. The specifics of which rivals made these accusations, and the exact context in which they arose, were not detailed in the available reporting.

The dispute surfaces as AI firms, including Anthropic, lean further into using AI systems to accelerate their own internal research and development pipelines, a practice that has intensified scrutiny of how much human oversight remains in the design and deployment of increasingly capable models.

No further statements from Amodei elaborating on his position, nor responses from the unnamed rivals, were included in the source material. The report frames the disagreement as part of an ongoing conversation within the AI industry about the balance between automation and human control, a debate sharpened by the pace at which AI companies are now applying their own technology to the work of building future systems.
