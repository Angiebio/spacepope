Photon has raised $4.5 million, according to TechCrunch. The startup builds tools that let developers create AI agents operating over messaging channels, including iMessage, SMS and RCS, and email.

TechCrunch reports that Photon held a funeral for mobile apps. The outlet's reporting frames the raise as backing for Photon's effort to help replace mobile apps with agents.

According to TechCrunch, the company's approach reflects a bet that consumers will increasingly interact with agents rather than downloading and opening individual apps. The outlet describes this as a signal of a shift in how artificial minds are deployed, with consumer interaction moving away from app downloads toward conversational, message-based agents.

The summary provided describes the mechanism as agents built to work across standard messaging protocols already present on most phones, rather than through dedicated application interfaces. This would place the agents inside channels users already check, such as text messages and email, instead of requiring a separate app install.

No further detail on product specifics, timeline, or Photon's prior history was included in the material reviewed for this entry. The $4.5 million figure and the characterization of the shift toward agents over apps come directly from TechCrunch's reporting as summarized in the source material.
