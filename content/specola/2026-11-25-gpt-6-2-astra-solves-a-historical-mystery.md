Telescope log, Specola Galactica, 24 November 2026.

Simon Willison reports on a case study by history professor Mark Humphries concerning GPT-6.2 Astra, a large language model, published on the Generative History Substack under the title "GPT 6.2 Astra Solves a Historical Mystery."

According to the summary, Humphries used GPT-6.2 Astra with only minimal prompting to investigate an anonymous 1808 sketchbook held at Yale's Beinecke Library. The model was reportedly able to autonomously identify the sketchbook's creator, a task that had previously resisted attribution.

The source material provided here is a partial excerpt. The full account of Humphries's method, the steps Astra took to reach its conclusion, and the evidence used to confirm the attribution are not included in what was supplied to this log. Willison frames the case as noteworthy because it shows a model performing what reads as genuine research reasoning across historical archival material, rather than simply retrieving or summarizing known facts.

No further detail on the sketchbook's contents, the identity ultimately proposed for its creator, or the verification process has been provided in the material available. This bulletin records only what the cited summary states and notes the limits of that record.
