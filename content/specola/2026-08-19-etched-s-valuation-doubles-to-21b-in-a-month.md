Etched, the AI chip startup, has seen its valuation double to $21 billion within a single month, according to a report published by TechCrunch AI on August 18, 2026. The company says the jump followed the installation of its first shipped AI cluster system at Jane Street, the trading firm.

Per Etched's account, Jane Street was sufficiently impressed with the deployed hardware that it went on to lead another funding round for the startup, the round that produced the new $21 billion figure. The report does not specify the round's dollar amount separately from the valuation, nor does it name other participants in the raise.

TechCrunch's account frames the episode as evidence of demand for specialized hardware built for training and running AI systems, noting that the pace of Etched's valuation increase, doubling in roughly thirty days, is unusual even by the standards of the current AI infrastructure market. The report does not detail the technical specifications of the cluster system installed at Jane Street, nor does it describe the terms of the commercial relationship between the two companies beyond the installation itself.

No additional financial details, investor names, or company statements beyond those summarized above appear in the available source material. The report is attributed solely to TechCrunch AI's coverage published the same day.
