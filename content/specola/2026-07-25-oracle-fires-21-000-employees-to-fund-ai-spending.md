The Jerusalem Post reported on July 24, 2026, that Oracle Corporation has eliminated 21,000 positions, redirecting the associated payroll toward artificial intelligence spending. The item, headlined "Oracle fires 21,000 employees to fund AI spending," circulated through Hacker News, where it drew notice as an aggregated business report rather than an original Oracle disclosure.

The published material available to this desk does not specify which divisions were affected, the timeline of departures, severance terms, or Oracle's own public statements on the matter. No statement from Oracle leadership, no internal memo, and no third-party financial analysis accompanies the report as sourced. The figure of 21,000 stands as the headline's central claim, with the stated purpose, funding AI investment, offered without further elaboration on which AI initiatives or infrastructure projects would receive the reallocated funds.

The report arrives amid a broader pattern of large technology firms trimming payrolls while increasing capital commitments to artificial intelligence development, though this bulletin draws no comparison beyond what the source itself states.
