Developer Simon Willison highlighted a warning from Claire Vo, who runs ChatPRD, an AI platform that helps product managers "turn an idea into a spec." Vo detailed the issue in a YouTube video published November 20, titled "Token fraud is here and it's BAD," with an accompanying thread on Twitter/X.

ChatPRD has been operating since the GPT-4 era, and Vo has documented its development publicly for much of that period. According to the summary provided, her video describes token fraud as an active and damaging problem affecting AI platforms, though the underlying material available does not specify the technical mechanics of the exploit or the scale of losses Vo has reported.

Willison's post, published on his site on November 20, frames the matter as a notable new security concern, tying it to the broader economics of AI agent platforms, where usage is typically metered and billed by token consumption. The citation does not elaborate further on remediation steps Vo may have proposed or on responses from AI providers whose infrastructure underlies ChatPRD's service.

Readers seeking the full account of the fraud mechanism, its financial impact on ChatPRD, and any mitigation Vo has since implemented are directed to the original YouTube video and the linked Twitter post, both cited in Willison's writeup.
