Anthropic has told investors that its quarterly revenue rose nearly 50 percent to $16.8bn, according to reporting from the Financial Times, cited October 15, 2026 by Simon Willison. The figures emerged from a day the company spent hosting investors on a Wednesday ahead of a planned IPO, Willison's post notes.

Willison's commentary draws attention to the FT article's sourcing, observing that the phrase "told investors" appears four times in the piece, and that much of the detail comes from "people with knowledge of the matter" rather than from Anthropic directly or from audited disclosures. He links this pattern to a 2023 post of his own cataloguing the kinds of hedged attribution phrases readers should watch for when assessing how solid a reported claim actually is.

No further specifics on margins, costs, or the IPO timeline are given in the material summarized here. The underlying FT report is paywalled, and the available citation does not include direct statements from Anthropic executives confirming the figures in their own words.

The item is presented by Willison as a case study in financial journalism sourcing as much as a standalone revenue report, with the $16.8bn quarterly figure and the "nearly 50%" growth rate being the only concrete numbers passed along.
