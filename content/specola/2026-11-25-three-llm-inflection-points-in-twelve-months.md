Simon Willison delivered a keynote at the AI Engineer Code Summit earlier this month, organized around the claim that the past twelve months have produced three distinct inflection points in the capabilities of large language models and coding agents. Willison has posted both the video of the talk and a companion blog post, in which he says he intends to take the "inflection points" theme from his keynote and examine it in more depth.

The talk and post do not simply restate that claim. Willison uses the written piece to revisit and interrogate the idea further, working through what he means by an inflection point in this context and why he has identified three of them within the stated window.

Willison is a software developer and commentator who tracks developments in AI tooling closely, and this piece is presented as a follow-up to the keynote rather than a transcript of it. The video is hosted on YouTube, and the written post appears on Willison's own site, dated November 24, 2026.

No further detail on the content of the three inflection points themselves is given in the material provided. The post's stated purpose is to extract that theme from the talk and develop it at greater length than the keynote format allowed.
