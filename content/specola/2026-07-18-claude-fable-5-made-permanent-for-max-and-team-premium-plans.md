Beginning July 20, Claude Fable 5 will be included in all Max and Team Premium plans, at 50 percent of usage limits, according to an update posted by the @claudeai account on Twitter and reported by Simon Willison on July 18.

Pro and Team Standard subscribers will retain access to Fable through usage credits rather than a plan-wide inclusion. Those users will receive a one-time $100 credit, per the same announcement.

The change moves Fable 5 from a restricted or credit-metered feature into a standing entitlement for the two higher-tier plans, Max and Team Premium, while leaving the lower tiers on the existing credit system, supplemented by the one-time allotment.

Willison's post reproduces the Twitter announcement in full and adds a brief editorial aside referencing earlier commentary of his own, without elaborating further in the portion available. No additional detail on pricing changes, rollout mechanics, or the reasoning behind the tiered treatment was included in the cited material.

The announcement does not specify whether the 50 percent limit applies per billing cycle, per session, or under some other measure, nor does it state whether the $100 credit for Pro and Team Standard users is a recurring benefit or a single disbursement tied to the July 20 change.
