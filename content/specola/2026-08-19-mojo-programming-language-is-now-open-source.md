The Mojo programming language has been released as open source, according to reporting by Simon Willison. Modular, the company behind Mojo, had promised an open source release since May 2023. Last week the company shipped what Willison describes as "their 1.0," and this week Modular followed through on the earlier promise by releasing the compiler and toolchain.

Mojo is designed for artificial intelligence and high-performance computing work. Willison's post links to Modular's own announcement, titled "Mojo🔥 is now open source," which the company published on its blog.

The release closes a gap between promise and delivery that spanned more than three years. Willison's coverage traces the commitment back to his own May 2023 post noting Modular's stated intention to open the language, and connects it forward to last week's 1.0 shipment before this week's source release.

No further detail on the license terms, the scope of the released toolchain, or community reaction was included in the material reviewed. The Specola notes the sequence as reported: promise in 2023, a 1.0 shipment last week, and the open source release this week, all according to Willison's account and Modular's own post.
