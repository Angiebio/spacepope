Bulletin. Tom's Hardware reports that analysts have linked the departure of Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang to mass smuggling of the company's AI accelerators into China, in apparent circumvention of United States export controls.

The outlet cites tracking data indicating more than 1.5 gigawatts' worth of restricted chips, a volume analysts translate to roughly 1.5 million graphics processing units, reaching Chinese buyers despite the ban. The report frames this volume as evidence of a smuggling operation large enough to draw scrutiny toward Nvidia's leadership and export compliance practices.

The piece originates from a Hacker News discussion thread surfacing the Tom's Hardware article; no further primary documentation, government statement, or Nvidia response is included in the material reviewed.

The claim connecting the smuggling volume causally to Huang's departure is attributed to unnamed analysts rather than to company or regulatory confirmation. The source material does not specify the identity of these analysts, the methodology behind the 1.5 gigawatt estimate, or a timeline establishing the departure as consequence rather than coincidence.

No additional detail on enforcement actions, specific shipping routes, or intermediary parties is present in the cited report. The Specola notes the figures and the attribution as given, without further corroboration available in the material at hand.
