On August 27, 2026, TechCrunch reported that OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and more than 100 other companies and AI startups issued a joint call for action to defend against rogue artificial intelligence.

According to the report, the coalition characterized the current state of cybersecurity as inadequate to meet a new generation of cyber threats. The companies used the announcement to advertise a solution they say can help ward off these threats.

TechCrunch's summary names OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google specifically among the signatories, with the outlet reporting that more than 100 additional companies joined the call.

The report frames the announcement as both a warning and a promotional moment, with the assembled companies presenting themselves as the source of a proposed remedy to the security gap they describe.
