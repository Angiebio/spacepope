Simon Willison's site on September 7 published an excerpt attributed to Jakub Pachocki, laying out what Pachocki calls the strongest argument for continuing to train much smarter models quickly. The quoted passage, sourced to OpenAI's own published material at openai.com/index/an-alien-mind, frames rapid capability advancement as a defensive necessity rather than a race for its own sake.

According to the excerpt, the reasoning runs as follows. Powerful AI systems will be needed to defend against dangers posed by other AI systems. Pachocki specifies three defensive functions such systems would serve, securing infrastructure, protecting against rogue agents in real time, and inventing new protective measures not yet conceived. The passage states this defensive application "will be a primary focus of OpenAI's deployment efforts."
