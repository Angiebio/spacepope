The Debian Project has voted to permit what its resolution text calls "responsible use of generative AI" within the project, according to a report published by LWN and circulated on Hacker News on August 29, 2026.

The story material available to this observatory does not include the resolution's full text, the vote tally, or the discussion that preceded it. LWN's report is the sole cited source, and no direct quotations from Debian's mailing lists, its Technical Committee, or individual developers have been provided beyond the phrase named in the headline itself.

Debian is one of the oldest and most widely used Linux distributions, and its governance proceeds through general resolutions voted on by its developer body. A vote of this kind, if confirmed by the full resolution text, would mark a formal policy position on a question many free-software projects have faced informally: whether and how contributors may use generative AI tools in writing code, documentation, or other project material.
