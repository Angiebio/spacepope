The claim embedded in the title, that DeepSeek has released an inference engine as open source, comes from the Hacker News headline alone and has not been independently confirmed by this office through repository contents or developer statement.

If accurate, the move would extend a pattern of open releases from DeepSeek, the Chinese AI developer whose earlier model releases drew wide attention for undercutting the cost structure of rival systems from Western firms. An inference engine, the software layer that runs a trained model's calculations to produce outputs, sits downstream of the model itself. Open-sourcing such a tool would lower one more barrier to running large models outside the control of their original developers.
