Simon Willison's weblog reported on November 9 the release of llm-anthropic 0.31, the plugin update marking Anthropic's launch of two new models, Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2. According to Willison's post, Fable 5.2 is currently ranked first on the Artificial Analysis independent benchmarking site.
