OpenAI announced price reductions across its GPT-5.6 model line on July 30, according to a report by Simon Willison. GPT-5.6 Terra received a 20 percent price cut, while GPT-5.6 Luna saw an 80 percent reduction, the steeper of the two changes.

In its announcement, titled "Advancing the price-performance frontier with GPT-5.6," OpenAI attributed the reductions to efficiency gains tied to a system it calls GPT-5.6 Sol. The company detailed the underlying technical approach in a companion post, "How GPT-5.6 fuses frontier intelligence with frontier efficiency," which describes the engineering behind the cost improvements.

Willison's summary, published on his blog, characterizes the Luna price drop as "massive" relative to the more modest Terra reduction, though the specifics of what distinguishes the Terra, Luna, and Sol variants within the 5.6 family were not fully detailed in the material reviewed.

The announcement did not specify exact dollar figures for the new pricing tiers, nor did it include an effective date beyond the July 30 posting. OpenAI's stated rationale rests on efficiency gains rather than competitive pressure, per the company's own framing in its technical post.

No independent confirmation of the underlying efficiency claims was available in the cited sources at time of writing. Further specifics on deployment scale or customer impact were not included in the reviewed material.
