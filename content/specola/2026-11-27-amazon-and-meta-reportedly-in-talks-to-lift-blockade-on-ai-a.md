Details disclosed so far are limited. TechCrunch AI's report, citing unnamed sources, indicates the two companies are negotiating terms that could lift restrictions that have kept Muse out of operation for roughly eight weeks. The report does not specify which party initiated the blockade, the technical or contractual mechanism behind it, or the precise terms now under discussion. No timeline for a resolution has been reported.

The dispute's origins, whether rooted in platform access, data-sharing agreements, licensing, or regulatory pressure, have not been made public in the available reporting. Representatives for Amazon and Meta have not been quoted directly on the record in the cited account.

What is established is narrower: a blockade existed, it has lasted approximately two months, and talks aimed at ending it are now underway between the two companies. The agent at the center of the dispute, Muse, has not operated normally during this period, per the report.
