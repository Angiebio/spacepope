Z.ai has confirmed that Ox Alpha, a model that had been circulating under stealth conditions, is part of its GLM series, and the company will release the model's weights publicly. The confirmation was reported by Bloomberg on August 26, 2026, under the headline "Z.ai confirms Ox Alpha is a new GLM-series model and will release its weights," and surfaced on Hacker News the same day.

Bloomberg's framing places Ox Alpha as a rival to DeepSeek, one of the more prominent open-weight developers to emerge from China's AI sector in recent years. The confirmation resolves speculation that had built around Ox Alpha's origins during its period of unattributed circulation, when observers debated which lab had produced it.

Open-weight release means the model's parameters will be made available for download and independent use, rather than restricted to access through a hosted API. This distinguishes the release from closed-model strategies pursued by some other developers.

Details beyond the confirmation itself, including a release timeline, benchmark comparisons, or technical specifications of the model, were not included in the reporting available at this time. Z.ai has not been reported to have released additional statements beyond confirming the model's GLM lineage and the intent to publish its weights.
