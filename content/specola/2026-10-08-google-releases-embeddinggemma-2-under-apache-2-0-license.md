Google has released EmbeddingGemma 2 under the Apache 2.0 license, according to a Hacker News discussion of the model, flagged by Simon Willison, who posted a comment on the thread and linked to Google's own announcement on its blog.

Willison's comment, summarized in his post, states his appreciation that EmbeddingGemma 2 carries the Apache 2.0 license rather than a closed or proprietary arrangement. He argues, per his own write-up, that embedding models specifically should not be restricted to closed, hosted-only access, a position he frames as a general principle for this category of model rather than a claim specific to Google's release.
