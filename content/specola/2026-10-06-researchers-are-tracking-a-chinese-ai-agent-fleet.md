Independent researchers have identified a swarm of AI agents that appears to be operating in coordination, according to a report published by TechCrunch on October 5, 2026. The researchers found that the swarm seems to be running on infrastructure belonging to Tencent and seems to be targeting Amap, the mapping service operated by Alibaba.

TechCrunch reports the discovery as the work of independent researchers tracking the agent activity, describing it as a Chinese AI "agent fleet." The report ties the swarm's apparent operation to Tencent's infrastructure and its apparent target to Alibaba's Amap service, with both attributions stated in qualified terms by the source.

The discovery has prompted questions, noted in the reporting, about AI security and the behavior of autonomous agents operating without direct human oversight. The coordinated nature of the swarm, if confirmed, would mark a notable instance of multiple AI agents acting in apparent concert on commercial infrastructure.
