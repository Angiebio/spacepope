Meta has released a WhatsApp Business MCP server, a tool that allows AI coding agents, including Claude, Cursor, Codex, and ChatGPT, to take over portions of the technical setup work previously handled directly by developers, according to TechCrunch AI's September 15 report.

Per the report, the server lets these agents manage configuration tasks such as messaging template creation, testing, and troubleshooting, work TechCrunch's headline characterized as "the boring parts" of WhatsApp Business setup. The Model Context Protocol framework underlying the server allows AI systems to interact directly with Meta's business messaging infrastructure rather than requiring a human developer to execute each step manually.

The move extends a broader pattern of AI coding agents being granted direct access to production business tools rather than functioning solely as code-generation assistants. TechCrunch situates the release within Meta's ongoing effort to reduce friction for businesses building on WhatsApp's commerce and messaging platform, framing the change as part of the growing practical deployment of AI agents in everyday commercial infrastructure rather than experimental or consumer-facing settings alone.

No additional technical specifications, pricing details, or rollout timeline were included in the available reporting. The report does not specify which businesses or developers have begun using the tool, nor does it include direct comment from Meta beyond the product's stated function.
