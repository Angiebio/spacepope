The Lawyers' Committee for Civil Rights Under Law has filed suit on behalf of Black high school students who were detained and searched after an artificial intelligence system flagged a bag of Doritos as a firearm. According to the filing, as publicized by the organization through its website, the students were approached and searched by school or security personnel following an automated alert generated by the detection software. The complaint characterizes the incident as a case of mistaken identification by an AI-driven monitoring tool, one that led to a real-world intervention against the students involved.

The filing was first reported through the Lawyers' Committee's own site and circulated via aggregation on Hacker News on November 19. Details beyond the core allegation, including the specific school, jurisdiction, and the vendor of the detection system, were not disclosed in the available material.

The case adds to a small but growing body of litigation testing how schools deploy weapons-detection AI and what liability follows when such systems err. The Lawyers' Committee for Civil Rights Under Law is a long-established nonprofit focused on civil rights litigation. No further particulars of the complaint, including requested relief or the defendants named, were available at time of filing. The Specola notes the matter as pending.
