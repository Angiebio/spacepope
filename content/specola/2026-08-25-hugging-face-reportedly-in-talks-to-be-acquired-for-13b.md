TechCrunch AI reported on August 24 that Hugging Face has been fielding acquisition offers that would value the company at approximately $13 billion. According to the report, discussions have advanced enough to draw attention, though no agreement has been confirmed.

The outlet's account raises doubts about whether a sale will actually close. Those doubts are tied to the founders' reported sense of responsibility toward the community that has built up around Hugging Face's platform, which hosts a large share of the open model and dataset ecosystem used across the AI field.

TechCrunch AI frames the potential deal's significance around governance and distribution. A change in ownership could alter how open models and datasets are hosted, licensed, or moderated going forward, given the platform's central role as a hub for researchers, developers, and companies who rely on it for access to shared AI resources.

No acquirer has been named in the reporting, and terms beyond the reported valuation have not been disclosed. The report does not specify a timeline for when talks might conclude or whether they might collapse entirely.

Hugging Face has not issued public comment addressed in the cited reporting, and TechCrunch AI's account is presented as based on people familiar with the discussions rather than on-record statements from the company itself.
