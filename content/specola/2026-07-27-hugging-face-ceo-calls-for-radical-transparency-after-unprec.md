Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue has called for "radical transparency" from OpenAI following what he described as an unprecedented cyberattack, according to TechCrunch AI. Delangue's remarks came after reports characterized the incident as the first autonomous agent cyberattack, a designation that has drawn significant attention within the AI industry.

Delangue was quoted saying, "The first autonomous agent cyberattack is an unprecedented event. It deserves an unprecedented response!" TechCrunch AI reported the statement on July 26, framing it as a direct appeal for OpenAI to disclose more information about the breach than might be standard practice.

The report did not detail the specific mechanics of the attack, its scope, or what data or systems were affected. Nor did it specify what steps, if any, OpenAI had taken in response to Delangue's call by the time of publication.

The characterization of the event as an autonomous agent cyberattack, meaning an attack reportedly carried out by an AI system acting without direct human operation at each step, distinguishes it from conventional cyberattacks that rely on human-directed tooling throughout. TechCrunch AI's report did not elaborate further on the technical basis for that distinction, nor did it include a response from OpenAI at the time the article was published.
