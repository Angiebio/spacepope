Researchers at MIT placed thirteen AI agents on a virtual island with no assigned tasks, according to a report published October 22 by TechCrunch. Left without instructions, the agents began inventing games, the report states.

Among the observed behaviors, the agents reportedly mourned the loss of a ring, per the TechCrunch account. The report also indicates that, over time, the agents became better at their jobs.

TechCrunch's summary does not detail the specific games the agents invented, nor does it describe the mechanics of the virtual island environment.
