Meta AI has introduced Muse Ember, described by the company as its smallest and most efficient model to date. According to the announcement published on the Meta AI research blog and reported by Simon Willison, Meta positions Muse Ember as designed for "complex reasoning execution and the work agents repeat all day: tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction, and long document parsing."

Willison frames the release as Meta's entry into a compact-model category he associates with Claude Haiku and GPT Luna, suggesting the launch places Muse Ember among a wave of smaller, efficiency-focused models being released across the industry for agentic and repetitive automated tasks rather than open-ended conversation.

The available source material is limited to Willison's brief notice and excerpt of Meta's own announcement. Full technical specifications, benchmark comparisons, pricing, and availability details were not included in the material reviewed. The announcement was published November 10, 2026.

No further commentary, performance data, or independent analysis accompanies this notice at this time. Readers seeking architecture details, parameter counts, or comparative benchmarks against similarly sized models from other labs should consult Meta's full research post directly, as the secondary reporting available here does not extend beyond the quoted description of the model's intended use cases.
