Anthropic has merged its Claude Cowork product with the standard Claude chat interface into a single unified offering, the company announced in a blog post titled "Claude Cowork and chat are now one Claude." According to Anthropic's announcement, the change addresses user confusion over the distinction between Cowork, chat, and Claude Code.

The company's blog post describes the merged product as capable of handling both quick queries and longer-running tasks, stating that a user can "bring a quick question, or hand over a report due at noon, and Claude takes it from there, even after you've closed your laptop." Anthropic indicates the rollout is beginning with Pro-tier subscribers, though the source material provided does not specify the full rollout schedule or timeline for other subscription tiers.

Simon Willison, writing on his blog, noted that he had personally found the prior split between Cowork and chat interfaces increasingly difficult to navigate, and characterized the consolidation as welcome news on that basis. Willison's post links to and quotes from Anthropic's original announcement but does not provide additional independent reporting on the technical mechanics of the merger.

The available material does not detail what became of the Cowork branding beyond the merger, nor does it specify changes to pricing or feature sets accompanying the interface consolidation. Further particulars of the rollout were not included in the cited sources.
