A coalition describing itself as scientists, legal scholars, and technology leaders has publicly disputed Anthropic's characterization as a safe artificial intelligence company, timing the challenge ahead of the company's anticipated public offering.

The statement, titled "Anthropic Is Not a Safe AI Company," was distributed November 18 via Business Wire and circulated on Hacker News. According to the release, the coalition is contesting the safety branding Anthropic has built its public identity around, arguing that the company's actual practices do not match its stated commitments.

The provided material does not specify the coalition's membership roster, the precise evidentiary claims underlying the challenge, or what remedies, if any, the group is seeking. Anthropic's response, if one has been issued, is not included in the source material.

The timing, ahead of a reported initial public offering, places the dispute squarely in view of investors and regulators assessing the company's governance claims. Anthropic has marketed itself since its founding as an organization built around AI safety research, a positioning that has shaped its reputation among researchers, policymakers, and the public.

No further detail on the coalition's formal demands, the specific incidents or policies cited, or Anthropic's timeline for responding is available in the cited material. The story is developing, and subsequent statements from either party were not part of the record reviewed here.
