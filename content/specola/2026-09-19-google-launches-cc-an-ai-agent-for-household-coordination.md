Google has introduced CC, an AI agent repositioned toward household coordination, according to TechCrunch AI (September 18, 2026). The outlet reports that families can share emails, schedules, and tasks with the agent, which then manages calendars, fills out forms, compiles shopping lists, and plans meals.

TechCrunch frames the move as a refocusing of the CC product, suggesting Google is directing the tool's capabilities toward the coordination work of running a home rather than more general-purpose assistance. The report describes the agent's access spanning multiple categories of family logistics at once, from correspondence to scheduling to provisioning, consolidated under a single point of delegation.

No pricing, availability timeline, or technical specifications beyond these described functions were included in the available source material. The report does not specify which households or regions will see the feature first, nor does it detail safeguards around the shared email and schedule access the system requires to operate.
