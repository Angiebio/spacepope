OpenAI introduced two new models on November 17, GPT‑6.2 Astra and GPT‑6.2 Sol, according to a company announcement cited by Simon Willison. OpenAI describes Astra as its new frontier model, intended for the hardest projects, while Sol is positioned as an efficient, versatile model for everyday work.

Both models are available through OpenAI's API under the identifiers gpt-6.2-astra and gpt-6.2-sol, per the announcement.
