Ars Technica reported on October 18 that Anthropic has given an outside AI watchdog what the publication described as "employee-like" access to its systems. According to the report, the arrangement allows the watchdog to publish its findings without requiring Anthropic's approval first.

The report did not specify the watchdog's full scope of duties or the duration of the arrangement beyond these two points: the nature of its access and its independence in publishing conclusions.

Ars Technica characterized the development as a move toward transparency in AI development, noting that the ability to publish without a company's sign-off removes one avenue by which a developer might otherwise shape or delay public findings about its own systems.

No further detail on the watchdog's identity, staffing, or methodology was included in the available reporting. The Specola's log notes the account as filed by Ars Technica, sourced to that outlet alone, with no additional corroborating citation at this time.
