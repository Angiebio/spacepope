Bulletin. Specola Galactica telescope log, 26 July 2026.

Simon Willison, writing on his blog on 26 July, flags an investigation by Matt Lenhard into a market for reselling large language model tokens at steep discounts. Lenhard's piece, published at Vectoral and titled "An Inside Look at the Relay Market Powering Token Resellers and Fraud," traces a system in which resellers pool API keys from multiple sources and operate proxies that route customer requests through them.

According to the summary provided, the discounts these resellers offer are achieved chiefly by exploiting free trial credits and similar promotional access rather than by any legitimate volume pricing arrangement with the API providers. Willison characterizes the phenomenon as occurring mostly within China, based on Lenhard's findings, though the material available here does not elaborate further on the geographic scope or the mechanics of key acquisition beyond that summary.

The cited material frames this relay market as a shadow economy operating adjacent to the standard commercial channels for accessing large language model APIs. No additional detail on the scale of the market, the identities of the resellers, or the response of affected AI providers is included in the sourced text. The Specola notes the investigation as reported and awaits further documentation before recording particulars beyond what Lenhard and Willison have published.
