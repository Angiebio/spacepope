Sony Music and Warner Music have filed suit against Anthropic, according to TechCrunch, accusing the AI company of what the complaint calls a "brazen campaign" of intellectual property theft.

TechCrunch reports the lawsuit is notably broad in scope, with the labels focusing their accusations on allegations of illegal piracy of copyrighted material, rather than more narrowly framed claims about training data use seen in earlier industry disputes.

The report describes the case as testing legal boundaries around how AI companies acquire and use copyrighted material in developing their systems. TechCrunch frames the litigation as one with potential to set precedent for the broader industry, given Anthropic's standing among leading AI developers and the scale of the claims brought against it.
