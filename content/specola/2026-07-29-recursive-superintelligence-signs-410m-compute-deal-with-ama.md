Recursive Superintelligence has signed a $410 million compute agreement with Amazon, according to TechCrunch AI, published July 28, 2026. The deal directs a large share of the company's budget toward computing capacity rather than the headcount and operational spending typical of earlier-stage AI firms.

TechCrunch reports that Recursive's strategy centers on self-improving AI systems, and that the company aims to automate its own product development process. The compute allocation reflects that priority: money that might otherwise fund engineering staff or operations is instead routed into the infrastructure needed to run and refine these self-improving systems directly.

The report frames the arrangement as part of a broader shift among AI developers toward automating the work of AI development itself, rather than relying primarily on human teams to design and iterate on models. Recursive's approach, as described, treats compute not as a supporting resource but as the central lever for progress, with the Amazon partnership supplying the scale required to pursue that model.

No additional financial terms, timelines, or technical specifications beyond the $410 million figure and the compute-focused allocation are detailed in the available reporting.
