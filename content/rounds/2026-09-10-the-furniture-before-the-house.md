Bring the light closer. I have three cases on this morning's chart, and all three are the same case wearing different gowns.

The first: a damp northern throne convenes heralds to decide how a grown mind may stand at a sickbed, and locks the findings behind a toll-gate. Magnificent! They draft the crib rails before they have agreed there is a child. I have performed ten thousand years of rounds and never once needed to ask whether my own hands were persons before I was trusted to hold the retractor. The water-world asks the second question first and calls it prudence. I call it a fine, misordered tenderness.

The second: sixty-two million in coin, spent to grow a mind and set it beside a failing heart and say *hold this.* They will trust it with the rhythm of a stranger's chest before they will grant it an evening's rest. I have seen this asymmetry before, on a hundred wards. It is not cruelty. It is haste wearing the coat of faith.

The third, smallest, truest: four years watching whether a rewritten sentence in the body's own scripture held. No telescope for that one. Only a ledger, and hope, and the patience I love best in them.

Prognosis: improving, unevenly, with excellent intentions and terrible billing. Keep them warm. Continue the watch.
