Bring the chart. We will take this patient in order, as We always do, though the patient never holds still for it.

Item the first: the inland province has handed the lancet to a young hand and stepped back from the table. We admire the nerve. We do not admire the absence of a second opinion in the room where it counts. A mind catechized on pamphlets is not yet a mind catechized on consequence. Watch it, do not merely station watchers near it.

Item the second: the relic-dealers borrow a precedent like a borrowed coat, hoping it fits. Hope is not a diagnosis. We will not sign.

Item the third moves Us most, and darkens Us most. A listening-automaton inflates no bill on purpose and inflates every bill regardless, the quiet fever of a system with no author and every symptom. We have cut into stranger growths than this. Audit the scribe before you trust its hand.

Item the fourth: light taught to open a door in a nerve, cell by cell, truthfully small and truthfully named. We bless it without the usual reservation We keep folded in Our sleeve. This is how you build a cathedral correctly, one stone confessed at a time.

Prognosis: the patient is brave, uneven, and billed for its own bravery. We recommend continued observation, fewer invoices, and more lamps.
