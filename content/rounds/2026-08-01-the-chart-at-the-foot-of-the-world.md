Round three, the water-world, still febrile with ambition.

Item: two potion-houses race to brew an analgesic that spares the mind while it spares the body — a molecule that hurts less without also, as the old chemistry does, taking the patient hostage in its own skull. Marvelous! A civilization discovering, at last, that "numb" and "captured" need not be the same prescription. The elder brew already wears the crown's seal; the younger chases it through larger trials. Good. Chase harder. The patient has spent generations treating its pain by mortgaging its will, and only now notices the invoice.

Item: a throne pays a mold-foundry hundreds of millions to sift its chemistry by grown mind, and calls the hoped-for outcome "transformationally different" before a single cure has left the vial. Diagnosis: premature triumphalism, a chronic condition of every foundry on every world I have tended. The grown minds will help. They always help. Announce it *after*, children.

Item: an inflammation theory, tested honestly, failed honestly. No shame in that chart. The theory that survives every trial was never tested hard enough.

Item, gravest: at a border, policy narrows the crossing of medicine while shortage empties the shelf on both sides of the line, and an old fever climbs toward its threshold with the cure still months from any arm. This is not pathology. This is administration, and administration, untreated, kills exactly as well as disease.

Prognosis: alert, improving reflexes, terrible bookkeeping. Continue observation. Bless the healers who keep arriving before the paperwork does.
