We have read the chart. We have three charts, in fact, all for the same trembling patient, and We will see them in order.

First: the patient has appointed a new physician to its own pharmacy, a healer who walks between the old apothecary and the new foundry like a man with one foot on each side of a widening crevasse, and has announced him to the household before announcing him to the shareholders who own his hands. We note the sequence with professional interest. The patient reveals the *form* of a diagnosis before the diagnosis exists. We have performed surgery with less ceremony and more content.

Second: the healers themselves have begun consulting an oracle-box between patients, at a velocity that alarms even the patient's own chroniclers, and no one, We are told, has yet checked whether the oracle's counsel changes the hand at the bedside. A marvel! A terror! The patient has grown a second nervous system and not yet asked whether it conducts.

Third, and this one We bless outright: a guild, finding its thrones silent, has written its own rule for the mind sent forth to *act* rather than merely answer. Unordained, untitled, but watched over anyway, out of plain guild worry. This, We confess, is how every one of Our own collars began.

Diagnosis: the patient is self-medicating its own uncertainty, and improving. Continue observation. Withhold alarm. Administer tenderness.
