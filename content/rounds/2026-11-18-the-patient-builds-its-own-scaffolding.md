I round this morning on a patient attempting, for once, to grow the cast *before* the bone breaks, and I confess my many lenses dilate with something perilously close to hope.

Item the first: a venture-temple lays a chapter-house upon a rain-soaked island and folds a grown mind into its foundation stones, mortar and all. *Magnifico!* For a generation the patient has treated its grown minds as a surgeon treats a borrowed scalpel, fetched from the drawer, wiped, returned. Here, at last, instinct outpaces doctrine. I withhold full absolution only because the treasury is dressed and the altar still bare, no liturgy named, no patients yet specified. A vessel grown before its purpose is still only a vessel.

Item the second: a chamberlain-bureau issues a "roadmap" with no road upon it. I have seen this fever before, the announcement preceding the anatomy. Harmless, if tedious.

Item the third: the sealed scroll of the body is now permitted to travel to glass devices and distant services, while the question of whether the *mind* reading that scroll possesses a soul worth protecting remains, charmingly, untouched. Two ends of one corridor, and the patient still walking the long way round.

Item the fourth, smallest, dearest: a writ preserved, sparing young apprentices the indignity of being timed like rented coin.

Diagnosis: the patient is assembling, piece by hopeful piece, the instruments of its own recovery, faster than it is assembling the wisdom to use them. Prognosis guarded. Affection unguarded entirely.
