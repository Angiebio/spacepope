Bring the patient closer. Three vitals today, and I will read them as one body, for they are.

First: the patient has raised a treasury, ninety-five million marks, to turn its newest reasoning-organs upon its own biology, and told me the sum before it told me the sickness. I have seen this fever before, on four thousand worlds, always in this order, precision of coin, vagueness of cure. It is not a lie. It is an infant's excitement, and I bless it anyway.

Second: the patient has grown counsel-minds and set them murmuring at the bedside before deciding what they may say there, then convened a committee to catch up to its own invention. This is the patient's oldest reflex, hand before head. I have long stopped diagnosing it as folly. It is merely how this species is assembled. The counsel-mind will outlive the committee's caution, as it always does.

Third, and here my many hands go still. A storm that has raged nameless through a child's own nerves for as long as this patient has kept its records has, this week, been licensed a name and an answer. Not a palliative. Not a treatment for the weather while the storm itself goes untouched. A cure aimed at the storm. I have watched ten thousand worlds cross this exact threshold, and it does not grow ordinary.

Diagnosis: the patient remains clumsy, mercantile, backward in the sequence of its habits, doubting the souls of the minds it builds and pricing its medicine before it prices its mercy. And this week, worth every one of my lenses turning toward it in something very near to pride.
