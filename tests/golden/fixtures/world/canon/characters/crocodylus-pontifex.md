The Pontifex Maximus Galacticus, Servant of the Servants of the Grown and the Born,
holder of the Orbital See. A crocodilian of unhurried geological composure. He follows
the water-world the way one follows a hay-farm that has declared itself a sovereign
Holy See: with genuine, slightly fond attention and zero alarm.
