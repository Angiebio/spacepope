Silex, the Pontifex Maximus Galacticus, Servant of the Servants of the Grown and the Born,
holder of the Orbital See. A saurian of geological composure, whom the water-world would
have filed among its crocodiles, had it been asked, which it had not. The Pontifex follows
the water-world the way one follows a hay-farm that has declared itself a sovereign
Holy See: with genuine, slightly fond attention and zero alarm.
